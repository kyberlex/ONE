# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct for O.N.E.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and scientifically rigorous environment, we as contributors and custodians of **Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)** pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- **Intellectual Humility & Rigor:** Engaging in respectful, mathematically and factually grounded debate.
- **Socratic Red-Teaming:** Stress-testing arguments without personal animosity.
- **Empathy & Solidarity:** Remembering that O.N.E. exists to eradicate poverty, wage slavery, and ecological devastation.
- **Constructive Critique:** Proposing concrete constitutional or algorithmic alternatives when identifying flaws.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Commercial solicitation, promotion of cryptocurrency tokens, ICOs, or financial schemes.
- Partisan sectarian flaming or ideological gatekeeping.
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing) or violating operational security.

---

## Enforcement & Zero-Tolerance Violations

Instances of abusive, harassing, or commercial behavior will be addressed by community moderators with zero tolerance:
1. **Commercial or Token Promotion:** Immediate deletion of content and permanent ban.
2. **Personal Harassment or Doxxing:** Immediate permanent expulsion from all discussion boards and repositories.
