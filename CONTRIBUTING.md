# Contributing to Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)

Welcome to **Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)**. This repository contains the canonical constitution, mathematical invariants, simulation engines, multi-language thrillers, and DIY hardware blueprints for a post-monetary, automated civilization.

O.N.E. is an open civilizational operating system. It is governed not by corporate boards, political parties, or charismatic leaders, but by open-source thermodynamic invariants and civic sortition.

---

## 1. The Core Invariants (Non-Negotiables)

Before contributing or opening proposals, ensure your contribution strictly complies with the **Class-0 Hard Invariants**:

1. **The Non-Commercial Purity Invariant:**  
   O.N.E. will never issue a token, sell a subscription, accept fiat donations, or gate knowledge behind paywalls. All contributions are gifted unconditionally to the planetary commons under open-source and Creative Commons licenses.
2. **The Zero-Monopolization Invariant:**  
   Basic survival needs (clean water, nutrient-dense food, ecological housing, healthcare, electrical power) are birthrights inalienable from any human being, governed by thermodynamic usufruct.
3. **The Anti-Oligarchic Sortition Invariant:**  
   Governance cannot be concentrated in electoral political parties or permanent administrative hierarchies. Legislative and adjudicative councils rotate strictly by statistical lottery (sortition).

## 2. Where and How to Contribute

### A. The Dialectic Forum & Open Frontiers (GitHub Discussions)
For philosophical debates, speculative ideas, local chapter organizing, and general feedback on the novels, use [**GitHub Discussions**](https://github.com/kyberlex/one/discussions):
- 💡 **OIP & RFCs:** Deep constitutional or algorithmic refinement proposals.
- 🔬 **Constitutional Debates:** Stress-testing the 46 Canonical Articles.
- 🌱 **Seed-Nodes & Physical Pilots:** Blueprints for solar microgrids, hydroponics, and mesh networks.
- 💬 **General & Fiction Readers:** Discussions on the 6-volume thriller series.

### B. O.N.E. Improvement Proposals (OIP / RFCs)
If you identify a genuine systemic gap (an unresolved Class-1 operational protocol, an unmodeled friction in input-output matrices, or an ambiguity in article implementation):
1. Review the existing 46 Articles in [`bible/ONE NETWORKED EARTH (O.N.E.).md`](bible/ONE%20NETWORKED%20EARTH%20(O.N.E.).md) and the simulation logs in [`oasis/oasis.md`](oasis/oasis.md).
2. Open an **OIP Issue** using our structured proposal template.
3. Submit formal simulation runs or plain-language specifications.

### C. Translations & Internationalization
O.N.E. is currently published in 15 languages (English, Italian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Arabic, Bahasa Indonesia, Turkish).  
Corrections to grammar, cultural nuance, and audio synchronizations are welcome via Pull Requests.

## 3. Pull Request Guidelines

1. **Clear Scope:** Keep PRs focused on a single article, translation chapter, or simulation script.
2. **Zero Commercial Links:** PRs containing donation links, commercial affiliations, or cryptocurrency addresses will be rejected automatically.
3. **Respectful Rigor:** We welcome fierce intellectual skepticism and red-teaming. We do not tolerate personal attacks, ad hominem insults, or tribal partisan culture wars.
