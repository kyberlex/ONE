# Contributing to Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)

Welcome to **Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)**. This repository contains the canonical constitution, foundational invariants, and dialectic frameworks for a post-monetary, automated civilization.

O.N.E. is an open civilizational operating system. It is governed not by corporate boards, political parties, or charismatic leaders, but by open-source thermodynamic invariants and civic sortition.

---

## 1. The Core Invariants (Non-Negotiables)

Before contributing or opening proposals, ensure your contribution strictly complies with the **Class-0 Hard Invariants**:

1. **The Non-Commercial Purity Invariant:**  
   O.N.E. will never issue a token, sell a subscription, accept fiat donations, or gate knowledge behind paywalls. All contributions are gifted unconditionally to the planetary commons under open-source and Creative Commons licenses.
2. **The Zero-Monopolization Invariant:**  
   Basic survival needs (clean water, nutrient-dense food, ecological housing, healthcare, electrical power) are birthrights inalienable from any human being, governed by thermodynamic usufruct.
3. **The Anti-Oligarchic Sortition Invariant:**  
   Governance cannot be concentrated in electoral political parties or permanent administrative hierarchies. Legislative and adjudicative councils rotate strictly by statistical lottery (sortition).

1. Review the existing 46 Articles in [`ONE NETWORKED EARTH (O.N.E.).md`](ONE%20NETWORKED%20EARTH%20(O.N.E.).md) and the interactive AI challenge on [open-networked-earth.surge.sh/#challenge](https://open-networked-earth.surge.sh/#challenge).

3. Submit concrete mathematical formulas, plain-language legal articles, or thermodynamic parameters.

1. **Constructive Rigor:** We welcome fierce intellectual skepticism and adversarial stress-testing.
2. **Zero Commercial Solicitation:** Any promotion of cryptocurrency tokens, ICOs, fee-based services, or commercial schemes will be removed with zero tolerance.
3. **Respectful Dialogue:** Attacks on ideas are encouraged; personal insults or tribal partisan flaming are strictly forbidden.
