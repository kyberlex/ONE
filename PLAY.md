## **Why O-ASIS Exists**

Traditional city-builders and economic simulations (SimCity, Factorio, Anno) train players to accumulate infinite fiat money and treat the natural biosphere as an infinite extraction dump.

**O-ASIS inverts this paradigm:**
1. **No Money, Pure Thermodynamics:** All activity is governed by strict physical conservation laws: **⚡ Solar kWh**, **💧 Filtered Water Liters**, **🥗 Food Calories** (2,200 kcal/die biometric floor), and **💻 Mesh Compute MFLOPs**. Matter and energy cannot appear from nothing.
2. **Second-Law Entropy:** Machines wear down. Solar inverters degrade and pumps require maintenance. Materials must be recycled through the circular FabLab shredder.
3. **The Legacy Adversary AI:** An algorithmic opponent that simulates modern financial extraction—executing debt strikes, shutting down utility grids, imposing audits, and offering predatory bailouts.
4. **Athenian Demarchy (Sortition Councils):** Community dilemmas are not resolved by autocratic dictators or career politicians, but by randomly selected citizen juries (strict odd parity: 3 or 15 citizens) with rotating mandates.
5. **The Dual-Track Handshake (Open Hardware):** Advancing in the game unlocks real-world, verified engineering blueprints: parametric 3D printable CAD files (`.STL` and `.3MF` for hydroponics) and tested **Home Assistant YAML automations** (Zigbee/MQTT) for off-grid battery load-shedding.
6. **Serverless P2P Multiplayer & Sovereign Whisper:** Connect with other player settlements via WebRTC invite links with zero central servers, featuring end-to-end encrypted chat (**ChaCha20-Poly1305**) running client-side in the browser.

## **Field Manual & Controls**
* **F1 or Rulebook Icon:** Opens the complete in-game survival guide and thermodynamic formulas.
* **1 / 2 / 5 / Space:** Game speed controls (1x, 2x, 5x, Pause).
* **Mouse Drag / Wheel:** Smooth pan and zoom across the planetary hex grid.
* **Wrench Icon:** Opens the FabLab & Dual-Track 3D CAD/YAML export studio.
* **Assembly Icon:** Inspects the active Athenian sortition assembly and citizen dockets.
