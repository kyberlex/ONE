#### **BACK COVER TEXT:**

At the exact moment Detroit roars awake at **05:42 UTC**, the freezing mountain gale howls through the rugged freight yards of the **Val di Susa** at the mouth of the Fréjus Alpine Tunnel. Court receivers and militarized private rail security surround the decommissioned locomotive sheds. The bankruptcy liquidation warrants have cleared Frankfurt and Milan. The scrappers are moving in to strip the hydro turbines.

Inside the cavernous brick roundhouses are four hundred alpine residents whose heating gas was cut by utility monopolies, and an emergency mountain rescue clinic caring for hypothermic elders and children.

Holding the line are two relentless pioneers:  
**Giulia Bellini**, a top Milanese insolvency litigator who fled her corporate firm after discovering her restructuring orders froze mountain communities to death, now hunted across Schengen under an emergency European arrest warrant.  
And **Matteo Rossi**, a fifty-one-year-old hydro-electric locomotive mechanic with scarred forearms, early silicosis, and a third-generation contempt for banking technocrats.

This mountain hub is Node 02 of **O.N.E. (Open Networked Earth)**.  
Governed by an immutable constitutional invariant: **Zero lethal force. Bodily integrity absolute.**

To defend the valley against corporate siege and freezing starvation, they turn the mountain itself into an impregnable sanctuary: eighty-ton gravity-switched rail cars blocking the tunnel throat, high-head water deflectors from glacial flumes, refurbished Pelton runners powering four thousand freezing homes, and an optical infrared laser transceiver firing over the 3,500-meter peaks of Mount Rocciamelone directly into Swiss civil trusts.

As European banking syndicates face a systemic debt freeze and thousands of French, Italian, and Swiss citizens converge upon the alpine pass, a seven-day confrontation begins that will shatter private property dogma forever.
