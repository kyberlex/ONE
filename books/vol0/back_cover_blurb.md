#### **BACK COVER TEXT:**

Eighteen months before the synchronized global ignition of Day Zero, ten hunted fugitives across four continents receive an anonymous, encrypted lifeline. An ex-Wall Street quant facing federal wire fraud indictments; a Beijing cyberneticist fleeing state security after leaking classified aquifer collapse reports; a Mumbai dockworker organizer dodging corporate assassination squads; an exiled Korean cryptographer blacklisted by military intelligence; a Viennese jurist who walked away from the European Court.

To each, an anonymous architect offers the impossible: unconditional sanctuary in the only place on Earth quantum surveillance and mercenary hit teams cannot reach—**Station 74-Nord**, a decommissioned Cold War acoustic submarine detection vault carved 250 meters deep into solid granite beneath an Arctic fjord in Spitsbergen.

They arrive in staggered secrecy, stepping out of the polar blizzard into the bunker's warm tungsten glow, meeting each other for the very first time. They expected an empty hideout to ride out their warrants. Instead, they find ten prepared bunks, a closed-loop geothermal microgrid, a mechanical 1958 Siemens teletype wired to a cryogenic optical mainframe, and an unlocked heavy steel airlock.

The door has no lock from the inside. Any of them can walk away at any second. But outside lies an uninhabited, -40°C Arctic wilderness, and on the bulkhead, an analog chronometer ticks: **720 hours until the sea ice freezes the fjord solid**, cutting off their only extraction trawler until the following summer.

When the mechanical teletype suddenly clatters to life in the dark, it does not issue threats. It reveals why these ten specific people were gathered: together, their minds represent the complete nervous system of human civilization—macro-finance, state cybernetics, grassroots labor, indigenous commons, international law, thermodynamics, and cryptography. The terminal poses the question that will either unite them or destroy them:

*The old world is mathematically doomed to collapse within eighteen months. If you do not design its successor, who will?*

What follows is not a polite academic seminar, but a fierce, claustrophobic psychological crucible (*12 Angry Men* meets *Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy* beneath the Arctic permafrost). Through screaming matches, panic attacks, the seductive trap of a "virtuous technocratic exam," and the table-flipping fury of class rebellion, ten strangers must forge the tools of human liberation: pure statistical sortition, the inviolable sanctity of the human body, and the restorative silence of the Founders' Covenant.
