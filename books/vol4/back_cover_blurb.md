#### **BACK COVER TEXT:**

At the exact second the network ignites across three continents at **05:42 UTC**, the digital iron curtain drops across the world’s busiest maritime chokepoint. Algorithmic predictive enforcement grids and corporate credit monopolies execute a synchronized freeze: the digital identities of one hundred thousand dockworkers and commercial sailors are erased in a split-second. Frozen wallets. Revoked biometric credentials. Autonomous surveillance drones idling above the container stacks.

Inside the rust-stained graving docks of a decommissioned shipyard overlooking the dark waters of the Malacca Strait, there are no black-ops saboteurs. There are stranded merchant crews abandoned on foreign shores, structural welders nursing arc-burned corneas, and working-class families seeking shelter inside reinforced sea containers.

Standing against the algorithmic panopticon are two relentless renegades:  
**Captain Jin Wei**, a 52-year-old heavy marine diesel chief with a spine ruined by three decades in engine rooms, blacklisted across the Pacific after defying corporate orders to scuttle a listing freighter with its crew aboard.  
And **Dr. Lin Zhang**, a 38-year-old algorithmic architect who designed the predictive scoring engines for Singapore's financial conglomerates—until she realized her models were being used to starve dissidents into submission, sending her fleeing into the harbor mist with the cryptographic seed keys.

This shipyard is Node 04 of **O.N.E. (Open Networked Earth)**.  
Protected by an absolute constitutional invariant: **Zero lethal force. No guns drawn. Bodily integrity and privacy inviolable.**

To hold the docks against private maritime security task forces and jamming swarms, Jin and Lin weaponize the brutal physics of global trade: sixty-meter rail gantry cranes dropped into lockdown under deadweight gravity, industrial ballast pumps discharging high-pressure water curtains that blind thermal targeting optics, and an encrypted Free Space Optics infrared laser mounted atop the shipyard crane firing pulses across international shipping lanes directly into the global mesh.

As container freight rates freeze on global exchanges and private gunboats close in on the graving dock, an open-sea confrontation begins that will liberate the global commons once and for all.

**A pulse-pounding, high-seas technological thriller of breathtaking speed and emotional depth.**  
*168 HOURS. ONE VITAL STRAIT. ONE UNBROKEN EARTH.*
