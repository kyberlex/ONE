#### **BACK COVER TEXT:**

At the exact second Detroit's laser pierces the Michigan sky at **05:42 UTC**, the freezing desert gale howls across the salt flats of the Andean Altiplano. Armored gun-trucks belonging to transnational lithium consortia seal off the regional aqueduct gates. Five thousand indigenous families turn their taps to find only dry dust. Their drinking water has been auctioned off on the Chicago futures exchange to wash lithium brine.

Deep inside the decommissioned ventilation tunnels of an abandoned copper mine 13,000 feet above sea level, there are no armed insurgents. There are silicotic miners, grandmothers boiling the last clean drops of water, and an indigenous community refusing to be wiped off the map.

Standing between the corporate mercenaries and the dry valley are two scarred fighters:  
**Carlos Quispe**, a 54-year-old Aymara deep-hole driller whose cracked knuckles and quiet rage carry the memory of a daughter lost to arsenic contamination, trusting nothing but heavy steel pipe wrenches and ancestral stone flumes.  
And **Sofia Arismendi**, an elite Santiago water-rights litigator who spent fifteen years locking down indigenous aquifers for foreign mining cartels—until the human cost broke her conscience, sending her on the run across the mountains with the offshore registry keys.

This subterranean stronghold is Node 03 of **O.N.E. (Open Networked Earth)**.  
Bound by an unbreakable constitutional law: **Zero lethal force. No firearms drawn. Bodily integrity inviolable.**

To survive the siege and unlock the watershed, Carlos and Sofia turn the geology of the Andes into an impregnable thermodynamic redoubt: gravity-tripped gravel sluices that halt armored convoys dead in their tracks, deep-shaft geothermal condensation coils pulling drinkable moisture from volcanic air, and an encrypted optical laser transceiver firing across the snow-capped cordillera into the sister nodes of Detroit and the Alps.

As global lithium stocks shudder on Wall Street and mercenary contractors prepare to breach the shaft entrance, a seven-day war of attrition begins that will forever dismantle the privatized ownership of the Earth.

**A pulse-pounding, high-altitude ecological thriller grounded in forensic geology and human heart.**  
*168 HOURS. ONE CONTINENT. ONE UNBROKEN EARTH.*
