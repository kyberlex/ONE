## **1. Hard Disqualification Criteria ("Red Flags" — Immediate Reject)**

Do **not** acquire or lease any parcel exhibiting even one of the following conditions:
1. **No Independent Water Right / Depleted Aquifer:** Absence of either perennial surface/spring flow, $>600\text{ mm/yr}$ harvestable precipitation with cistern storage permits, or verified potable borehole yield ($\ge 15\text{ L/min}$ continuous).
2. **Legacy Industrial / Heavy-Metal Soil Contamination:** Proximity ($<2\text{ km}$ downstream) to tailings dams, petrochemical sites, or PFAS plume zones (must pass ISO 11047 heavy-metal laboratory screen).
3. **100-Year Floodplain or Active Landslide Toe:** Parcel building envelope sits inside FEMA/EU 100-year flood inundation zones or unstable $>25^\circ$ clay slip planes.
4. **Restrictive Building Ban with Zero Reversible-Chassis Exemption:** Jurisdiction prohibits both permanent agricultural/cooperative dwellings *and* reversible helical-pile modular units (`MHU`).

## **2. Weighted 100-Point Field Scoring Matrix**

A candidate parcel must achieve **$\ge 75 / 100\text{ points}$** (and zero Red Flags) before executing the [`foundation/CROSS_BORDER_SEED_NODE_CHARTER.md`](../../foundation/CROSS_BORDER_SEED_NODE_CHARTER.md):

| Category | Physical / Legal Metric | Verification Method | Max Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Hydrological Autonomy** | Spring/well static water table $<40\text{ m}$ depth + annual rainfall $\ge 650\text{ mm/yr}$ + gravity head for cisterns. | 72-hour pump drawdown test + local meteorological 30-yr series. | **25 pts** |
| **2. Agronomic & Soil Health** | Topsoil A-horizon depth $\ge 25\text{ cm}$, organic matter $\text{SOM} \ge 3.0\%$, $\text{pH } 5.8-7.2$, $\ge 0.12\text{ ha}$ arable per resident. | 5-point auger soil core lab assay (N-P-K, CEC, heavy metals). | **20 pts** |
| **3. Renewable Exergy Potential** | Unshaded southern (or northern in S. Hemisphere) solar horizon $\ge 1100\text{ kWh/kWp/yr}$ + micro-hydro or wind complement. | PVGIS / Global Solar Atlas horizon profile + shading audit. | **20 pts** |
| **4. Legal & Zoning Permissibility** | Mixed agricultural/forestry/eco-hamlet zoning allowing reversible helical-pile timber MHUs and greywater reed beds. | Written municipal zoning certificate (*Bauvoranfrage* / *Certificat d'urbanisme*). | **20 pts** |
| **5. Logistics & Mesh Line-of-Sight** | All-weather road access for $3.5\text{ t}$ timber delivery + line-of-sight for Reticulum LoRa / fiber backhaul. | Physical truck access check + RF Fresnel zone elevation profile. | **15 pts** |
