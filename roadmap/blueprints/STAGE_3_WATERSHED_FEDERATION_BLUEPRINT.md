## 1. Objectives & Bioregional Scale
An isolated node is vulnerable to localized ecological disasters, legal encirclement, or genetic bottlenecks. In Stage 3, the embryonic node interconnects with 3 to 5 neighbouring nodes within the immediate watershed (a 15–50 km radius) to form a **Federated Bioregional Commons**. Trade, communications, and dispute resolution occur directly between nodes without reliance on state telecommunications, central banks, or corporate supply chains.

### 2.1. The Reticulum Network Stack (RNS) & LoRa Mesh Architecture
* **Hardware:** Low-power solar microcontrollers (ESP32 / RAK Wireless 4631) paired with 868 MHz (Europe) / 915 MHz (Americas) 1W RF transceivers and high-gain directional Yagi or omnidirectional fiberglass collinear antennas mounted on ridge tops.
* **Cryptographic Protocol:** The Reticulum Network Stack (RNS) provides end-to-end encrypted packet transport with zero centralized servers, zero IP addresses, and zero telecom sim cards.
* **Traffic Capabilities:**
  1. *Real-time telemetry:* Battery state of charge, cistern water levels, weather station alerts.
  2. *Asynchronous store-and-forward messaging:* Encrypted node-to-node email, dispatch notices, and mutual-aid requests.
  3. *Sortition Synchronization:* Distributed deterministic sortition seed verification.

## 3. Physical Thermodynamic Barter (The Kantorovich Exergy Matrix)

Within the federated watershed, fiat money is completely banned from inter-node settlements. Goods and services are balanced using **Thermodynamic Exergy Accounting (Art. 3.1)**:

$$\text{Exergy Value } (B) = \int \text{Megajoules of direct solar/fuel input} + \text{Tool depreciation (hours)} + \text{Ergonomic labor strain}$$

### 3.1. Non-Monetary Swap Examples
* **Node Alpha (Valley Farm):** Ships 500 kg of winter squash and 200 L of cold-pressed sunflower oil to Node Beta.
* **Node Beta (Timber & Mill):** Delivers 15 m³ of dimensional white oak lumber and 5 tons of seasoned firewood to Node Gamma.
* **Node Gamma (Fab-Lab):** Machines precision bronze bushings, turns replacement lathe spindles, and welds hydraulic loader buckets for Node Alpha.
* **Accounting:** Accounts are maintained on an open, append-only cryptographic ledger synchronized via the mesh radio, clearing seasonal balances through reciprocal physical work parties.

## 4. Legacy Utility Severance Protocol

When all primary life-support systems (water, electrical, food, heating, sewage) demonstrate unbroken reliability across three consecutive winter cycles:
1. **Notice of Service Termination:** The node serves formal written notice to municipal/private electric and water utilities requesting physical meter removal and final bill settlement.
2. **Physical Air-Gap Disconnect:** All grid-tied conductors entering the property are physically severed or routed through a lockable, visible-blade disconnect switch with a padlocked air-gap.
3. **Tertiary Standby Contingency:** If legal zoning mandates an active grid connection, the utility drop is capped with an automatic transfer interlock set to drawing 0 kWh/month, serving purely as legal compliance armor.

## 5. Federated Governance & Regional Sortition Juries

To prevent charismatic local leaders from entrenching authoritarian enclaves:
* **The Inter-Node Jury Protocol:** Any disciplinary action involving member expulsion, usufruct revocation, or major resource reallocation must be ratified by an **Inter-Node Sortition Jury**.
* **Jury Composition:** 5 members drawn deterministically from active residents across neighbouring sister nodes who have zero familial or financial interest in the dispute.
* **Proceedings:** Convened in person or via encrypted mesh radio, adhering strictly to the 5-Minute Structured Cooling Session rules.
