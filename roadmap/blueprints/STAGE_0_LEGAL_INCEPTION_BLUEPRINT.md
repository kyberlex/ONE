## 3. Two-Tier Legal Shield Incorporation Guide

To prevent commercial liabilities, taxes, and supplier disputes from touching the land:

```
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │    ENTITY A: PASSIVE LAND TRUST         │
                      │  (CLT / Stiftung / Communal Commons)    │
                      │ - Holds fee simple land title           │
                      │ - Absolute statutory asset lock         │
                      │ - ZERO commercial trading / debt        │
                      └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                           │
                                           │ 50-99 Year Usufruct Lease
                                           │ (Subordinated to Covenant)
                                           ▼
                      ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                      │    ENTITY B: OPERATING COOPERATIVE      │
                      │   (ULCAA Worker Co-op / Genossenschaft) │
                      │ - Holds trade tools & vehicles (UCC §9) │
                      │ - Conducts freelance ETI commerce       │
                      │ - Absorbs commercial and tax liability  │
                      └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1. Entity A: The Passive Land Trust
* **Common Law (US / UK / Canada / Australia):** Incorporate a 501(c)(3) Community Land Trust (CLT) or Perpetual Purpose Trust (PPT). Recorded deed includes:
  1. *Perpetual Usufruct Restriction:* Land may never be mortgaged, subdivided, or sold for speculative gain.
  2. *Notice of Non-Responsibility:* Entity A’s fee title is insulated against mechanics' liens incurred by Entity B.
* **Civil Law (Continental Europe / Switzerland):** Incorporate a non-profit Foundation (*Gemeinnützige Stiftung* / ZGB Art. 80 ff.) with immutable asset lock (*Vermögensbindung*). Establish a municipal compliance escrow to prevent administrative code fines (*Zustandsstörerhaftung*) from becoming senior liens (*Sicherungshypothek*) in the *Grundbuch*.
* **Customary Commons (Latin America / Agrarian Reform):** Inscribe agrarian title through an *Asamblea de Formalidades Especiales* and register with the National Agrarian Registry (RAN) under *Ley Agraria* Arts. 45/46. This eliminates precarious *posesionario irregular* status and bars arbitrary eviction.

### 3.2. Entity B: The Operating Cooperative
* Incorporate as a Worker-Owned Cooperative (Uniform Limited Cooperative Association - ULCAA, or European *Genossenschaft* / *Verein*).
* **The Zero Commercial Debt Rule:** Entity B is strictly prohibited from incurring un-hedged supplier debt or senior bank loans. All tooling must be acquired via 100% pre-funded escrow or pre-paid member patronage vouchers.
* **Demountable Trade Fixtures (UCC § 9-604 / ZGB Art. 677):** All commercial tools and machinery deployed by Entity B remain demountable tenant personalty on non-penetrating pads with quick-disconnects. If Entity B faces trade default, creditors can only repossess tenant tools, leaving the land untouched.

## 4. Zoning Stealth & Siting Protocols

* **Common Law:** Deploy road-legal, DOT-certified demountable mobile chassis (ANSI A119.5 Park Model RVs / tiny homes on wheels with functioning axles and tow hitches). They are legally classified as personal property (*personalty*), exempt from permanent IRC residential building codes. Agricultural utility structures are certified under farm exemptions (IBC § 105.2).
* **Civil Law:** Phase 0 acquisition must select parcels with grandfathered residential structures (**Bestandsschutz**). New workshops are erected strictly as permitted agricultural storage (*landwirtschaftliche Nebengebäude* under § 35 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 BauGB). Sanitation utilizes DIBt-certified closed holding tanks (*abflusslose Sammelgrube* per DIN 1986-100) with municipal pump-out manifests.
* **Customary Commons:** All living quarters and domestic dwellings must be sited within the registered *Zona de Urbanización Ejidal* (*solares urbanos*). The productive commons (*Tierras de Uso Común*) are strictly reserved for non-residential tool sheds (*casetas de aperos*) and agroforestry plots.

## 5. Governance & Dispute Prevention Protocols

1. **The 3-Person Sortition Mediation Panel:**
   - Any resident experiencing an unresolvable grievance pulls 3 active node members by deterministic lottery.
   - The panel convenes within 48 hours for a mandatory 5-Minute Structured Cooling Session (Art. 4.2).
2. **Written Guest & Resident Waiver Agreement:**
   - Every incoming resident signs an agreement explicitly waiving statutory residential tenancy rights and affirming the demonetized usufruct covenant.
   - Internal labor is legally structured under ULCAA / IRS Subchapter T as non-employee member-owner mutual aid (*nachbarschaftliche Hilfe*), defeating FLSA and MiLoG wage claims.
3. **Decoupled Commercial Directorship:**
   - Statutory commercial officers of Entity B are held by professional external trustees or limited liability agents, ensuring rotating communards are never exposed to personal tax liability.
