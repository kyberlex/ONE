### Mandate A: The "MHU Living + Auxiliary Shell" Separation (Crushing CapEx)
Historical intentional communities routinely succumb to the **Ruin Trap**: attempting to inhabit ancient, uninsulated stone ruins or farmhouses. Century-old masonry suffers from chronic capillary rising damp, black mold (*Stachybotrys*), radon infiltration, and immense thermal mass that demands unsustainable heating ($>250\text{ kWh/m}^2\text{year}$ vs. MHU’s $<15\text{ kWh/m}^2\text{year}$). Retrofitting ancient ruins to Passivhaus standards often costs more per square meter (€1,500–€2,500/m²) than precision digital prefabrication, draining founders' physical and financial reserves before a single garden bed is established.

O.N.E. resolves this through the **Bifurcated Infrastructure Doctrine**:
1. **Existing Masonry / Agricultural Shells (Auxiliary Function):** Acquired strictly for their **grandfathered zoning status** (*Bestandsschutz*, *núcleo rural*, or agricultural barn permits) and repurposed as unconditioned auxiliary space: machine shops, heavy CNC/welding bays, covered timber drying, seed banks, root cellars, and livestock housing.
2. **Modular Habitat Units (Living Function):** All domestic living, sleeping, cooking, and sanitary hygiene are hosted **exclusively within Modular Habitat Units (MHU)**. MHUs deploy on non-penetrating helical screw piles, guaranteeing non-toxic indoor air, zero capillary moisture, mold immunity, and plug-and-play standardized utility docking (380V/48V DC, dual grey/black drainage) from Day 1.

### Mandate B: The Orthogonal Climatic Stress-Test (The "Zero-Surprise" Invariant)

> **"These five locations were deliberately chosen to span five radically different planetary climatic zones so that the Modular Habitat Unit (MHU) and its standardized modules are rigorously tested under extreme, non-overlapping physical conditions. When O.N.E. scales horizontally and replicates across hundreds of new nodes, the open hardware and blueprints will face zero unmodeled vulnerabilities or engineering surprises."**

Before mass global propagation, the MHU’s hybrid timber chassis, CNC nodal gussets, airtight membranes, insulation cores, helical foundations, mechanical ventilation (HRV), and standardized 800×800mm utility docking interfaces must be subjected to real-world operational stress across the full planetary spectrum:
* **Oceanic Saturated Moisture:** Persistent liquid saturation, low winter sun, mold pressure (`NODE-01`).
* **High-Altitude Montane Shock:** Extreme day/night thermal cycling, intense UV, mountain slope gravity (`NODE-02`).
* **Tropical Monsoonal Deluge:** 95% continuous RH, termite pressure, flash flooding, agricultural smoke (`NODE-03`).
* **Equatorial Constant Solar:** 365 days of 12-hour direct overhead radiation, airborne dust, vector parasites (`NODE-04`).
* **Severe Continental Freeze:** -30°C polar vortex, heavy snow loads, sub-soil frost heave, paired with +38°C summers (`NODE-05`).

## 3. Global Candidate Nodes: Detailed Field Profiles

### NODE-01: Inland Rural Europe (Galicia, Spain / Central Portugal)
* **Climatic Stress Domain:** **Humid Oceanic Temperate (*Cfb*)**. Characterized by relentless Atlantic low-pressure fronts, persistent overcast days, and annual precipitation exceeding 1,400 mm.
* **Why Sited Here:** Demographically drained stone hamlets (*aldeas abandonadas*) available at nominal cost under Civil Law Two-Tier Shields (*Cooperativa Integral* / Land Foundation), preserving grandfathered residential footprints (*Bestandsschutz* / *núcleo rural*).
* **Bifurcated Deployment:**
  - *Stone Ruin:* Stabilized with basic timber rafters as an unconditioned machine shop, iron forge, and wet equipment bay.
  - *MHU Living Capsule:* Assembled on helical ground screws with a 300 mm dense-pack cellulose envelope ($U < 0.12\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$) and fixed south-facing brise-soleil.
* **Hardware Stress-Test Focus:** Validates that the vapor-permeable exterior timber envelope continuously vents moisture outward without interstitial condensation, completely defying the dampness and mold that destroy traditional regional houses.
* **Micro-Siting Safeguard:** Siting strictly prioritizes deep granitic valleys populated by native deciduous oak/chestnut forests (*fragas* / *soutos*) with perennial headwater springs, permanently bypassing industrial eucalyptus wildfire corridors.

---

### NODE-02: Andean Latin America (Southern Ecuador: Azuay / Loja / Vilcabamba)
* **Climatic Stress Domain:** **Subtropical High-Altitude Montane (*Cwb* - 1,800 m to 2,200 m)**. Intense equatorial solar irradiance by day, immediate radiative cooling to single digits by night.
* **Why Sited Here:** Land cost per hectare is exceptionally low, held under inalienable Customary Commons & Indigenous Usufruct (*Beni Comunitari / Cabildo Comunal* per ILO Convention 169). Natural building materials (adobe, raw clay, cane, stone) zero out auxiliary storage costs.
* **Bifurcated Deployment:**
  - *Adobe Shell:* Dry seed bank, root storage, and communal tool library.
  - *MHU Living Capsule:* Lightweight digital chassis on screw piles, utilizing local cane (*carrizo*) and raw sheep wool for lightweight insulation.
* **Hardware Stress-Test Focus:** Tests thermal expansion and contraction tolerances in CNC timber joints under brutal diurnal thermal shock. Verifies that high-altitude UV radiation does not degrade exterior sealants, while high-head gravity springs deliver continuous 5+ bar static water pressure without an electrical pump.
* **Micro-Siting Safeguard:** Siting strictly bounded between 1,800 m and 2,200 m to avoid high-altitude hypobaric hypoxia (>3,000 m), while requiring an independent geological survey to steer clear of active volcanic alluvial pathways.

### NODE-03: Rural Southeast Asia (Northern Thailand / Laos Highlands)
* **Climatic Stress Domain:** **Tropical Monsoon (*Am*)**. Extreme tropical deluge cycles, sustained ambient temperatures above 32°C with 90%+ relative humidity, and fierce biological decomposition pressures.
* **Why Sited Here:** Registered long-term usufruct deeds (30–60 years renewable under *Sap-Sit-Thi-Kep-Kin*) cost modest capital. Abundance of giant timber bamboo (*Dendrocalamus asper*) allows erecting covered fab-lab pavilions for pennies.
* **Bifurcated Deployment:**
  - *Bamboo Pavilion:* Shaded, open-air workshop for heavy fabrication, welding, and carpentry.
  - *MHU Living Capsule:* Hermetically sealed capsule on elevated screw stilts with 100 mm ventilated insulation cavities, dynamic solar louvers, and positive-pressure mechanical ventilation.
* **Hardware Stress-Test Focus:** 
  1. *Biological Defense:* Validates that the elevated screw piles and stainless-steel collars prevent subterranean termites from ever touching the timber chassis.
  2. *Atmospheric Cleanroom:* During the February–April **agricultural smoke season** (PM2.5 >300 µg/m³), the MHU functions as an airtight positive-pressure refuge using dual HEPA and activated-carbon HRV filtration.
* **Micro-Siting Safeguard:** Elevated hillside placement safe from seasonal monsoonal flash-flood catchments.

---

### NODE-04: East Africa / Rift Valley (Western Kenya Highlands / Southern Tanzania)
* **Climatic Stress Domain:** **Equatorial Subhumid Highland (*Cwb / Aw*)**. High-altitude plateau (1,600 m – 2,100 m) combining 365 days of relentless 12-hour direct overhead sun with cool, clear nights and seasonal airborne dust storms.
* **Why Sited Here:** Direct customary fiduciary agreements with traditional village councils. Bypasses predatory commercial banking, establishing direct thermodynamic exergy accounting (Article 3).
* **Bifurcated Deployment:**
  - *Rammed-Earth Shell:* Communal meeting hall, grain milling, and primary health clinic.
  - *MHU Living Capsule:* Precision elevated unit providing total hermetic vector isolation against malaria mosquitoes (*Anopheles*), jiggers, and scorpions.
* **Hardware Stress-Test Focus:** Verifies long-term polymer and elastomer resistance against continuous equatorial UV degradation. Validates cyclonic intake pre-filters that prevent fine volcanic dust from clogging HRV heat-recovery cores, while testing continuous 365-day solar PV baseload generation.
* **Micro-Siting Safeguard:** Dual title security verification combining national digital registry checks (e.g. ArdhiSasa) with formal elder council ratification.

### NODE-05: North America — Unzoned Rural County (Ozarks: Missouri / Arkansas)
* **Climatic Stress Domain:** **4-Season Severe Continental (*Dfa / Cfa*)**. Severe polar vortex deep freezes down to -30°C, heavy snow loads, and deep subsoil frost penetration, reversing in summer into humid +38°C heatwaves.
* **Why Sited Here:** Vast unzoned rural counties with zero building codes, no building inspectors, and total freedom to self-build with on-site milled pine/oak timber, biological reed beds, and off-grid DC microgrids ($2,000–$4,000/acre).
* **Bifurcated Deployment:**
  - *Timber Pole Barn:* Built from on-site felled timber to house machine lathes, tractor maintenance, and precision CNC routing.
  - *MHU Living Capsule:* Mounted on helical piles anchored deep beneath the local frost line.
* **Hardware Stress-Test Focus:**
  1. *Thermal Gradient Extremes:* Stress-tests the Passivhaus envelope ($U < 0.12\text{ W/m}^2\text{K}$) under a massive 45°C temperature differential between indoor living space and external polar vortex conditions.
  2. *Frost-Heave Immunity:* Verifies that helical piles completely resist the ground-heave cycles that shatter traditional concrete footings.
  3. *Vector Elimination:* Eliminates the crawlspace moisture, termite rot, and tick/chigger infestations that plague traditional Ozark cabins.
* **Micro-Siting Safeguard:** Siting on elevated ridges above karst sinkholes and river floodplains; sub-soil Canadian earth tubes for intake HRV pre-tempering.
