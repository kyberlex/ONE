## 1. The 10-Step Seed Node Startup Checklist

1. **Assemble the Founding Trio:** You need at least 3 people: one physical builder/mechanic, one legal/administrative lead, and one agricultural/systems organizer.
2. **Select the Jurisprudential Archetype:** Choose Common Law (CLT + Co-op), Civil Law (Stiftung + Co-op), or Customary Commons (Ejido / Communal Title).
3. **Scout Land with Water Invariants:** Acquire 2 to 5 hectares with senior unencumbered groundwater well rights or a perennial gravity spring. Zero land debt.
4. **Incorporate the Two-Tier Shield:** Register Entity A (Passive Land Trust) and Entity B (Operating Co-op). Record the Ground Lease with UCC § 9-604 fixture severance.
5. **Fund the Land via Equity-Free Capital:** Acquire title 100% via non-debt donations or purpose trust endowments. Ban commercial mortgages.
6. **Deploy Compliant Dwellings:** Use ANSI A119.5 road-legal mobile chassis (Common Law) or grandfathered residential dwellings (Civil Law) to bypass residential building codes.
7. **Install the Water & Power Core:** 40,000L subterranean cistern + deep-well pump with 48V DC manual transfer bypass + 48V LFP battery vault with BMS cold throttling.
8. **Cache the 180-Day Caloric Buffer:** Stock 180 days of bulk dry grains, legumes, fats, salt, and heirloom sprouting seeds per resident in sealed gamma-seal buckets.
9. **Enforce the Non-Waivable Chore Floor:** Every adult contributes 5 to 7 hours per week of core biological chores (compost, water, firewood). No cash buyouts.
10. **Establish the 15h/wk ETI Freelance Pipeline:** 15–20 hours/week of remote software/CAD/consulting generates fiat to pay property taxes and import raw hardware.

| Trap | How It Kills Nodes | The O.N.E. Invariant Solution |

## 3. Critical Sizing Quick Reference

* **Water Consumption Baseline:** 100 liters / person / day (drinking, cooking, basic hygiene, garden). 15 people = 1,500 L/day ➔ 40,000L tank provides a **26-day reserve with zero inflow**.
* **Electrical Storage Baseline:** 2.5 kWh / person / day. 15 people = 37.5 kWh daily consumption. 48V 280Ah LFP bank (14.3 kWh per pack, 3 parallel packs = 43 kWh) covers 24 hours of critical loads during total overcast.
* **Caloric Security:** 2,500 kcal / person / day ➔ 450,000 kcal per person for 180 days ➔ ~140 kg of dry-bulk grains/legumes and 15L oil per person.
