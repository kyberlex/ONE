### **PREAMBLE: OPEN-SOURCE COMMUNITY & DOCUMENTATION STEWARDSHIP**

Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.) treats documentation, contributor onboarding, and scientific community facilitation as first-class civic infrastructure alongside biophysical equations, simulation code, and constitutional law.

This Compact establishes the binding rights, responsibilities, and mutual protections governing **Open-Source Community & Documentation Stewards**.

---

### **ARTICLE I: COMMUNITY, DOCUMENTATION & EDITORIAL AUTHORITY**

1. **Community & Documentation Stewardship:** Stewards hold operational responsibility over public community forums (`r/OpenNetworkedEarth` and GitHub Discussions), maintaining contributor onboarding guides, upholding the Code of Conduct, and facilitating constructive technical dialogue.
2. **Ecosystem Liaison:** Stewards facilitate cross-disciplinary exchange across open-source and scientific communities (`Julia Discourse`, `OSArch`, `FreeCAD`, `Home Assistant`, `INET YSI`, etc.) to connect domain builders (Pillars 1–4) and independent critics.
3. **Editorial Autonomy:** Architectural review is strictly bounded to factual, thermodynamic, and constitutional accuracy, respecting independent editorial judgment in community moderation and documentation structure.
4. **Moral & Attribution Inviolability:** Each steward’s name or chosen pseudonym receives permanent co-attribution across repository records and institutional releases under CC-BY-SA 4.0 and AGPL-3.0.

---

### **ARTICLE II: SUSTAINABLE CADENCE & OPERATIONAL SCOPE**

To ensure long-term resilience and protect against burnout, stewardship is governed by sustainable open-source parameters:

1. **The Core Mandate:**
   - **Documentation & Onboarding:** Maintain clear technical onboarding documentation, FAQ syntheses, and working group indexes across the repository.
   - **Forum Facilitation:** Co-moderate GitHub Discussions and `r/OpenNetworkedEarth`, ensuring rigorous Socratic inquiry and routing technical proposals to **Pillar 1** (Biophysical Economics), **Pillar 2** (Governance & Sortition), **Pillar 3** (SIMONE Julia simulation), and **Pillar 4** (FreeCAD Open-BIM & Home Assistant YAML).
   - **Contributor Triage:** Welcome incoming open-source contributors and domain critics, guiding them to relevant modules and test suites.
2. **Sustainable Part-Time Rhythm (Phase 0):** Designed to be fully compatible with personal and professional life (~4 to 6 hours per week of asynchronous documentation and community facilitation).
3. **Explicit Exclusion of Commercial Chore:** Stewards' energy is reserved for genuine open-source collaboration and intellectual dialogue. They are strictly protected from:
   - Paid advertising, commercial growth hacking, and engagement-bait spam.
   - Crypto token shilling, financial fundraising, or monetized paywalls.
   - Administrative bureaucracy or proprietary platform lock-in.
4. **Orderly Succession (Demarchic Protocol):** If life circumstances require a Steward to step down, an orderly transition is conducted within the steward quorum, and the outgoing steward transitions to *Emeritus Steward* status with permanent, irrevocable attribution.
