## **1. Purpose of This Directory**

Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.) does not operate on corporate hierarchy, venture capital, or speculative tokenomics. It is built by a federated coalition of **Lead Co-Stewards**, **Open Collaborators**, and **External Adversarial Critics** across five specialized pillars.

Every file in `stewards/` is designed so that any incoming domain practitioner, open-source developer, or senior academic critic can move from **initial curiosity to concrete technical delivery in under 30 minutes**, governed by the **Anti-Stall Rule** (*"Earned by Delivery, Never Closed on Promises"*).

### **For Senior Domain Critics (Track B):**
1. Open [`CRITIC_BRIEFING_PACK.md`](CRITIC_BRIEFING_PACK.md) and jump directly to your domain section (15–30 minutes reading/execution time).
2. Post your falsification or failure mode in the corresponding GitHub Discussion or open an **Adversarial Critic Report** Issue.
3. You do **not** need to endorse O.N.E.'s philosophy; your sole mandate is to try to break its math, physics, or legal invariants.
