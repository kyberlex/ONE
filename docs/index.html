The Mathematical Proof: Why Social Democracy Dies With Wage Labor

              
Both Gemini and ChatGPT derived the macroeconomic identity of the sovereign welfare state to prove its structural breakdown when marginal labor costs reach zero:

              
              

                Ttotal = tw · W + tc · C + tk · Π
              


              

                

                  1
                  

                    Collapse of Aggregate Wages:
                    
In modern Nordic economies, direct labor taxes (tw · W) and worker-driven consumption taxes generate 60% to 75% of public revenues. As automation drives human labor costs to zero (MCL → 0 ⇒ W → 0), labor tax receipts vanish entirely: tw · W = 0.

                  

                


                

                  2
                  

                    The UBI-VAT Negative Loop:
                    
Without wages, household consumption must be funded by state cash transfers or UBI (C = U). If the state attempts to tax this consumption via VAT (tc ≈ 25%), net revenue is negative: Net = tc · U - U = -(1 - tc)U. The state merely recovers a fraction of the liquidity it injected.

                  

                


                

                  3
                  

                    The Capital Tax Dilemma:
                    
To fund civilization exclusively from corporate profits (Ttotal ≈ tk · Π), the tax rate tk must exceed 85–95%. In competitive automated markets where marginal costs are near zero, competitive prices also collapse toward zero, eliminating economic profit Π except in artificial monopolies. Aggressive capital taxation prompts flight or investment strikes.

                  

                


                

                  4
                  

                    The Post-Monetary Solution:
                    
Open Networked Earth avoids this breakdown by removing money entirely from the core metabolic metabolism: Metabolic Input ➔ Automated Capital ➔ Exergy / Material Flow ➔ Universal Usufruct ➔ Direct Provisioning. No tax extraction or debt servicing is needed.
