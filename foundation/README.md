# **O.N.E. FOUNDATION DIRECTORY**

Welcome to the institutional and legal documentation repository for **Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)**.

This directory holds the corporate, fiduciary, and legal blueprints required to anchor O.N.E.'s digital philosophy and publications into physical, banking, and land commons in **Switzerland** and internationally.

---

## **Key Documents**

- [`statutes_foundation_one.md`](statutes_foundation_one.md) — **Official Foundation Statutes & Deed (*Stiftungsstatuten*)**
  - Canonical English Articles of Association and complete notarizable Swiss-German Public Deed (*Öffentliche Stiftungsurkunde & Statuten*).
  - Perpetual Asset Lock (*Vermögensbindung*), Non-Commercial Purity, and Usufruct land stewardship under ZGB Art. 80–89bis.
  - Odd-number Board parity (3 to 7 members), 75% qualified supermajority quorum, honorary governance (*Ehrenamtlichkeit*), and Sortition Advisory Council.
  - Swiss tax-exemption covenants (*Steuerbefreiung*) and Federal Foundation Supervisory Authority (*ESA*) integration.
- [`legal_framework.md`](legal_framework.md) — **Master Legal & Operational Blueprint**
  - Complete operational roadmap: Starting with a **Swiss Verein (*Association*)** (CHF 0 capital) today, transitioning to a **Swiss Stiftung (*Foundation*)** later via statutory asset transfer (*FusG Art. 86 ff.*).
  - Banking compliance (Form K, Alternative Bank Schweiz, Cantonal Banks, Sygnum/AMINA).
  - Land acquisition protocols for seed micro-nodes (navigating Swiss BGBB rural land rules, *Baurecht*, and cross-border subsidiaries in Italy/France/USA).
  - Author privacy and collective imprint attribution model.
  - Ready-to-use Swiss *Verein Statuten* (Articles of Association) template.
