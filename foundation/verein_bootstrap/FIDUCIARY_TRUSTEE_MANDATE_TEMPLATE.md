## **1. Purpose & Operational Scope of This Mandate Agreement**

To constitute **Open Networked Earth Verein (O.N.E. Verein)** with a 3-person odd-parity *Vorstand* while strictly preserving:
1. **Tax Exemption Eligibility (*Ehrenamtlichkeit*, `DBG Art. 56 lit. g` / ESTV Circular No. 12):** Board service must be demonstrably honorary (pro bono), with zero director's salaries or profit participation;
2. **2-of-3 Multi-Signature Discipline (*Kollektivunterschrift zu zweien*):** Zero unilateral commitment or single-signature authority;
3. **Irrevocable *Foundation ONE* Asset Lock (*Unwiderrufliche Vermögensbindung*):** Binding legal obligation to transfer the accumulated CHF 50,000+ reserve into **Stiftung Open Networked Earth (Foundation ONE)** upon completion of the Genesis Lock; and
4. **Class-0 OpSec & Privacy Protection:** Strict confidentiality protecting the architectural founder (`Kyberlex <kyberlex@proton.me>`), contributors, and donors against doxxing or unauthorized identity disclosure,

each of the 3 initial Board Stewards (whether a **Swiss public-interest attorney / licensed fiduciary** or a **Pillar 1–4 Co-Steward**) executes this bilingual **Vorstands- und Treuhandauftrag (*Board Steward & Fiduciary Mandate Agreement*)** upon accepting election to the *Vorstand*.
