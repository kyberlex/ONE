## **I. PREAMBLE & JURISPRUDENTIAL ARCHITECTURE**

Physical Seed Nodes of Open Networked Earth (such as those located in Galicia, the Andes, the Rift Valley, or the Ozarks) inevitably exist within distinct, sovereign national legal jurisdictions.

To enable physical land stewardship and microgrid deployment without exposing local communities to financial speculation or corporate liability, O.N.E. employs a **Two-Tier Cross-Border Legal Topology**:

```

                                     ▼
 ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐

 └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

This Charter establishes the binding covenants, asset protections, and demarchic obligations connecting each local operational vehicle to Foundation ONE.

## **II. INVIOLABLE AFFILIATION COVENANTS**

### **Article 1: Perpetual Anti-Speculation Asset Lock**
1. Real estate, watershed catchments, utility spines, and renewable energy hardware acquired by or entrusted to the Local Seed Node are permanently removed from speculative commerce.
2. The Local Seed Node shall never:
   - Mortgage, pledge, hypothecate, or encumber real estate or common capital assets as collateral for speculative loans or commercial debt.
   - Distribute financial profits, capital gains, or real estate appreciation to individual members or third parties.
   - Convert common land into private fee-simple freehold lots.

### **Article 2: Dynamic Usufruct & "Use It or Lose It" Enforcement**
1. All residential dwelling units (Modular Habitat Units — MHUs) and productive garden allotments are governed strictly by the **Dynamic Usufruct Covenant** ([`foundation/USUFRUCT_COVENANT_TEMPLATE.md`](USUFRUCT_COVENANT_TEMPLATE.md)).
2. **Abandonment Reversion:** If a dwelling is continuously unoccupied for more than **180 consecutive days** without an active Sabbatical Lock registered in the local commons ledger, usufruct possession automatically reverts to the Local Housing Pool.
3. **Sabbatical Lock:** Any resident in good standing may register a Sabbatical Lock for up to **365 days** to travel, study, or support other Seed Nodes without forfeiting their dwelling.

### **Article 3: Democratic Sortition & Odd-Parity Governance**
1. The Local Seed Node shall not establish permanent political dynasties or executive oligarchies.
2. Internal dispute resolution, resource allocation prioritization, and municipal housing arbitrations are adjudicated by **statistically sorted citizen panels in strict odd parity** ($n = 3$ for minor mediations, $n = 15$ for neighborhood councils, $n = 101$ for bioregional assemblies), adhering to [`foundation/SORTITION_PROTOCOL_SPEC.md`](SORTITION_PROTOCOL_SPEC.md).
3. Major statutory decisions require a **75% qualified supermajority** of the assembled sortition jury.

### **Article 4: Open Engineering & Thermodynamic Conservation**
1. Physical constructions must utilize verifiable open-source engineering blueprints ([`habitat/`](../habitat/) and [`roadmap/blueprints/`](../roadmap/blueprints/)).
2. All environmental sensors must adhere to **Class-0 Biometric Privacy**: abiotic parameters only ($\text{CO}_2$, temperature, humidity, water levels, DC voltage); continuous audio capture or biometric tracking is strictly prohibited.
3. Energy and water balances must be logged in physical units (kWh, liters) and made available for mutual aid audits.

## **III. MUTUAL RIGHTS & FEDERATION ASSISTANCE**

By executing this Charter, the Local Seed Node obtains:
1. **Statutory Shielding & Legal Assistance:** Defense by Foundation ONE against predatory eminent domain or corporate legal aggression.
2. **Bioregional Exergy Clearing:** Right to participate in inter-node non-monetary physical swaps (Stage 3 Watershed Federation) calibrated via Leontief Input-Output balances (`simone/src/layer1_leontief.jl`).
3. **Open R&D Endowments:** Eligibility to receive grant disbursements secured by Foundation ONE from open-source research funds (NLnet, Horizon Europe, Prototype Fund).
4. **Permanent Attribution:** Listing in [`stewards/ROSTER_AND_LEDGER.md`](../stewards/ROSTER_AND_LEDGER.md) as a recognized pioneer node.

## **IV. DISSOLUTION & ASSET PRESERVATION (SAFEGUARD CLAUSE)**

In the event of insolvency, severe regulatory attack, or voluntary winding up of the Local Seed Node under domestic law:
1. All land titles, physical infrastructure, and common capital assets shall not be liquidated on the open auction market.
2. Assets shall transfer directly to Foundation ONE (or a designated sister public-benefit land trust in the same nation) to maintain perpetual common usufruct.
