### **RFC-001: Sortition Anti-Capture — Deliberative Bicameralism & Secret Ballot**
* **Category:** RFC / Constitutional Hypotheses  
* **Labels:** `area:governance`, `status:simulation-hypothesis`, `attack:001`  
* **Reference:** [`oasis/constitutional_attack_challenges.md`](../constitutional_attack_challenges.md#attack-001--sortition-capture), `SIM-QA-03`

```markdown
### Background & Problem Statement
In a 15-citizen sortition assembly with quarterly staggered turnover, a disciplined external faction of just 4 members ($k = 4$, 26.7%) can command a permanent veto over constitutional decisions ($\ge 75\%$) and easily sway 5 uncoordinated citizens to capture ordinary decisions ($\ge 60\%$) through pre-drafted dossiers and conformity cascades.

### Proposed Structural Patches
1. **Sortition Bicameralism:** The drafting body and the voting assembly are separated. The voting assembly never drafts original text.
2. **Secret Balloting:** Eliminates open floor conformity pressure and breaks cabal verification.
3. **Mandatory Adversarial Briefing:** Independent sortitioned Advocate General provides counter-dossiers.

### Deliberative Questions for the Community
- Should all ordinances require separate drafting juries, or only measures above a specific scope?
- How should secret ballots be audited for cryptographic fairness while preserving total anonymity?
```

* **Category:** RFC / Constitutional Hypotheses

* **Reference:** [`oasis/constitutional_attack_challenges.md`](../constitutional_attack_challenges.md#attack-002--free-rider-cascade), `SIM-QA-04`

```markdown
### Background & Problem Statement
The graduated sanction ladder relies on peer policing. When free-riding exceeds 12–18%, administrative case capacity is saturated ($\text{Cases} > \mu_{\text{max}}$). Enforcement latency causes a "sucker's payoff" cascade where sincere cooperators withhold labor.

### Proposed Structural Patches
1. **Automated Physical Contribution Accounting:** Replaces peer reporting with objective cryptographic check-ins at critical utility sites.
2. **Algorithmic Circuit-Breakers:** Automatic systemic downgrade of non-vital resource tiers when aggregate labor reserves drop, removing personal friction.

### Deliberative Questions for the Community
- How should physical contribution tracking protect neurodiverse citizens or those with fluctuating health from unfair penalties?
- What is the threshold for activating circuit-breakers without causing panic?
```
