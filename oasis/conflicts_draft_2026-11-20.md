## **Draft 1. Art. 3.1.6** *(conflict 11; SIM-QA-38 D-38.6)* — **second version approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(wording approved; not merged; the two compression disclosure lines accepted with it)*

**Current:**
> 6. Tier 1 Autarkic Closure: Viability requires more than non-negative gross output: an annual public audit must show that all sectors directly or indirectly supplying Tier 1 needs, including shelter and healthcare, meet full Tier 1 demand from local resources and installed capacity alone, with external inputs at zero and a published reserve margin.

**Draft, first version (120 words):**
> 6. Tier 1 Autarkic Closure: Viability requires more than non-negative gross output. An annual public audit measures how far the sectors that directly or indirectly supply Tier 1 needs, including shelter and healthcare, meet full Tier 1 demand from local resources and locally maintainable capacity alone. It publishes the gap and the reserve margin. Closure is the goal. Three external inputs are not a failure, when a panel drawn from outside certifies each for a stated term: solidarity for Tier 1; the compacts of Article 10.3.2, counted at the level of the bioregion; and imports while a node is being built, under a plan to end them. A node has failed only when Tier 1 is in fact not delivered.

**Draft, second version** *(shortened after the maintainer asked whether 120 words were the minimum; 96 words)*:
> 6. Tier 1 Autarkic Closure: Viability requires more than non-negative gross output. An annual public audit measures how far the sectors directly or indirectly supplying Tier 1 needs, including shelter and healthcare, meet full Tier 1 demand from local resources and locally maintainable capacity alone, and publishes the gap and the reserve margin. Closure is the goal. Only three external inputs are not a failure, each certified from outside for a stated term: Tier 1 solidarity; compacts under Article 10.3.2, counted per bioregion; and imports while a node is built, under a plan to end them.

**What the second version cuts:** connecting words; "a panel drawn" ("certified from outside" keeps the point; who certifies is Organic Law); the last sentence on failure, which used a word defined only in SIM-QA-35 point 6 (not merged) and belongs with that text. **What it adds:** "Only", so that the list of three is closed. No point of the first version is dropped except the failure sentence, moved to SIM-QA-35.

**How the decision is carried:** "measures how far" and "Closure is the goal" (a goal, not a condition); "publishes the gap"; the three exceptions, each certified from outside and for a term; the last sentence (failure is Tier 1 in fact not delivered, SIM-QA-35 point 6); "locally maintainable" (SIM-QA-31 point 6).

**Compression disclosure** *(instruction 8; each line needs the maintainer's decision)*:
| # | Point in the current text | In the draft | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | the audit "must show" full closure "with external inputs at zero" | the audit measures and publishes the gap; three certified exceptions | **weakened** |
| 2 | "installed capacity" | "locally maintainable capacity" | **narrowed** (stricter: a machine nobody local can repair no longer counts) |
| 3 | "a published reserve margin" | "publishes the gap and the reserve margin" | kept |

**Red team of the wording.**
* *"for a stated term".* The term can be renewed without end; the sentence gives no maximum. A maximum is a number for Organic Law, and the Constitution then has no bound for it (instruction 10). For the maintainer whether a bound in words is wanted (for example "not renewed without a new certification").
* *"while a node is being built".* No end is defined; a node can be "being built" for decades. The plan to end imports is the only limit.
* *"A node has failed only when".* The word "failed" appears nowhere else in the canon yet; it is used by SIM-QA-35 point 6, not merged. The sentence should merge with that text or lose the word.
* *Art. 3.5.2* refers to "the gaps found by the Autarkic Closure audit": consistent with a gap that is published.
* *No figure.* The level of closure and the donor share are `NOT_RUN` (SIM-QA-38 RED-3, RED-4).

**Word count:** current 54; first version 120 (+66); second version 96 (+42). Chapter III: canon 886 of 1,000, not recounted with pending dockets (SIM-QA-34 and 38 also ask for room).

## **Draft 2. Art. 8.4.3** *(conflict 10; SIM-QA-33 D-33.2)* — **approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(wording approved, with the choice made in drafting: it applies to every act a tribunal hears, physical violence included; the disclosure lines accepted with it; not merged)*

**Current:**
> 3. **Stage 3 (Sortition Arbitration Tribunal):** Binding adjudication by a Citizen Tribunal of fifteen (15) citizens via Fact-Centric Inquiry (*Inquisitio Facti*). Adversarial courtroom trials are abolished: proceedings evaluate strictly verifiable physical evidence and open logs.

**Draft:**
> 3. **Stage 3 (Sortition Arbitration Tribunal):** Binding adjudication by a Citizen Tribunal of fifteen (15) citizens via Fact-Centric Inquiry (*Inquisitio Facti*). Adversarial courtroom trials are abolished. Proceedings evaluate verifiable physical evidence, open logs, and messages or recordings a person gives of their own will, once audited as in point 5. Testimony counts only when such evidence or a second independent source confirms it.

**How the decision is carried:** "messages or recordings a person gives of their own will" (the maintainer's addition; nobody is made to); "once audited as in point 5" (the guard against fabricated material, Art. 8.4.5); the last sentence (testimony never alone).

**Choice made in drafting, for the maintainer.** The decision admitted testimony "for the listed non-physical acts". The draft admits it, with the same guard, **for every act a tribunal hears, physical violence included.** Reason: the list of non-physical acts is not in the canon yet (Art. 8.4.4 is not amended), so a sentence limited to it would refer to nothing; and a rule under which a threat can be proven by a witness and a blow cannot is hard to defend. This is wider than what was decided.

**Compression disclosure** *(instruction 8)*:
| # | Point in the current text | In the draft | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | "strictly verifiable physical evidence and open logs" only | personal messages and recordings, and corroborated testimony, are added | **weakened** |
| 2 | "strictly" | removed | an intensifier; the limit is now the closed list of what counts |

**Red team of the wording.**
* *"audited as in point 5".* Art. 8.4.5 admits forensic telemetry "solely if reproducible under open review". A private video is not reproducible; the audit can only test whether it was altered. Point 5 may need a word for this at merge.
* *"a second independent source".* Two witnesses who agree in advance are two sources. Independence is for the tribunal to weigh; no text secures it.
* *"of their own will".* A person can be pressed to hand over a device. The words bar an order; they do not bar pressure.
* *Recording as surveillance.* Following a person to record them is the profiling of Art. 3.4.4 (SIM-QA-41 point 1); it is not said here and rests on that article.

**Word count:** current 35, draft 63, change +28. Chapter VIII: canon 862, 902 with accepted dockets, of 1,000.

## **Draft 3. Art. 10.3.3** *(conflict 1, the point left open; SIM-QA-38 D-38.5, second half)* — **approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(direction and wording approved together; the disclosure line accepted with it; not merged)*

**Current:**
> 3. Hoarding life-support resources, forming cartels, or cutting off downstream water triggers automatic Systemic Decoupling (*Disiunctio Reticularis*): immediate suspension of inter-basin grid interties, transit corridors, and advanced medical buffers until reciprocity is restored.

**Draft:**
> 3. Hoarding life-support resources, forming cartels, or cutting off downstream water triggers automatic Systemic Decoupling (*Disiunctio Reticularis*): immediate suspension of inter-basin grid interties, transit corridors, and advanced medical buffers until reciprocity is restored. Decoupling never withholds what Tier 1 requires.

**Compression disclosure** *(instruction 8)*:
| # | Point in the current text | In the draft | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

With Tier 1 excepted, decoupling reaches Tier 2 and Tier 3 flows only. A basin that can live on Tier 1 can ignore it. `NARROWED`: the sanction is smaller, and that is the intent.

## **Draft 4. Art. 1.2.5, cases (e) and (f)** *(conflict 2; SIM-QA-33 D-33.3; a third version of Draft C of [`chapter_i_draft_2026-11-20.md`](chapter_i_draft_2026-11-20.md), which is itself not merged)* — **approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(wording approved; not merged; not to be merged before the closed list of SIM-QA-33 is written)*

**Current canon:** none. **Draft C, second version, cases (e) and (f):**
> (e) in custody for predatory violence resisting stabilization, ordered and periodically reviewed by a jury drawn by lot; (f) to carry out the ruling of a tribunal drawn by lot concerning violence;

**Draft, third version of the two cases, and one sentence added before "Law may narrow these cases":**
> (e) in custody, ordered and periodically reviewed by a jury drawn by lot, for predatory violence resisting stabilization, or for harm to one person repeated after a tribunal's ruling and a relocation; (f) to carry out the ruling of a tribunal drawn by lot concerning violence or such repeated harm;

> Opinion, belief and speech on public matters are never such harm.

**How the decision is carried:** step 1 of the ladder (exclusion from Tier 3) holds nobody and needs no case. Step 2 (relocation by ruling, enforced) is case (f), widened by "or such repeated harm". Step 3 (custody) is case (e), widened by its second ground; "after a tribunal's ruling and a relocation" carries the order of the ladder into Chapter I, so that custody can never be the first answer. The guard on opinion and speech is the added sentence.

**Red team of the wording.**
* *"harm to one person".* Not defined in Chapter I; the acts are the closed list of SIM-QA-33 (not drafted). Until that list exists the words are open. `RED_WINS` **until the list is written**: this draft should not be merged before it.
* *"one person".* A person who harasses a family, or a different person each month, is outside the words. Intended: the case is narrow. Recorded.
* *"speech on public matters".* Speech aimed at a private person (threats, messages after a request to stop) stays inside the case; criticism of a body or of a person's public acts is outside. The line between a public act and a private life will be argued.
* *Permanent.* Chapter I is closed after the lock (D-35.2): this ground of custody can be narrowed by law and never removed from the text.
* *Outside Chapter I, to draft next:* Art. 8.5.2 ("predatory violence") must name the second ground; Art. 10.4.3 (no "involuntary deportation") must allow relocation by ruling.

**Word count:** change +29 on Draft C, second version (193): **222**. Chapter I after the second pass stood at 1,624 (ceiling suspended): **1653**.

## **Draft 7. Art. 8.4.4** *(conflict 7, item 7c; SIM-QA-25, SIM-QA-33; from SIM-QA-36 RED-13)* — **first version not adopted; second version approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(see the end of this draft)*

**Current:**
> 4. **Strict Material Legality:** Tribunals possess jurisdiction exclusively over physical assault, somatic violation, enclosure, or ecological poisoning, barring ideological prosecution. Jurors are selected by sortition from adjacent districts.

**Draft:**
> 4. **Strict Material Legality:** Tribunals possess jurisdiction exclusively over physical assault, somatic violation, enclosure, or ecological poisoning, barring ideological prosecution. A sexual act of an adult with a person below the age set by Organic Law, never lower than sixteen (16), is somatic violation, whatever that person said. Jurors are selected by sortition from adjacent districts.

**How the decision is carried:** the act is named as "somatic violation", a head of jurisdiction the article already has, so no new head is added; "whatever that person said" (consent is no answer); the age is a number in Organic Law; "never lower than" is the bound the Constitution keeps (instruction 10).

**The figure.** Sixteen is a proposal, not a finding. *From memory and not checked:* ages of consent in national laws lie mostly between 14 and 18, with 16 common (Switzerland and the United Kingdom 16; Germany and Italy 14; France 15). The options are 14, 16 or 18 as the floor; Organic Law may set the age above the floor and never below it. No one who works with children has read this.

**Red team of the wording.**
* *"an adult".* The canon states no age of adulthood. Until it does, the first word of the rule is open. SIM-QA-29 (the civic register) sets an age for service; the two should agree.
* *Close in age.* A person of eighteen with a person of fifteen falls under the words. Many laws make an exception for a small difference in age. Not drafted: an exception lowers the protection and needs a decision of its own.
* *Two young persons.* Outside the words ("an adult"); left to SIM-QA-25.
* *Proof.* Draft 2 applies: testimony with corroboration.
* *Consequences.* The article gives jurisdiction and says nothing of what follows; the ladder of Art. 8.4 begins with "direct sitting", which Art. 8.3.1 lets the harmed person decline. A child should never be asked to sit with the adult. Not said here; for SIM-QA-25.

**Word count:** current 28, draft 56, change +28. Chapter VIII: 971 of 1,000 with Drafts 2 and 5.

**Second version, approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20.** The maintainer objected that age is not the right trigger: cognitive maturity is, and some persons of fourteen are more mature than others of eighteen. The red team answered that a pure maturity test puts the young person on trial and gives every accused adult the same defence. The version adopted keeps both: a low floor with no maturity argument, and above it a test of exploitation.

> 4. **Strict Material Legality:** Tribunals possess jurisdiction exclusively over physical assault, somatic violation, enclosure, or ecological poisoning, barring ideological prosecution. A sexual act of an adult with a person below the age set by Organic Law, never lower than fourteen (14), is somatic violation, whatever that person said; so is one that exploits the immaturity or dependence of a young person above that age. Jurors are selected by sortition from adjacent districts.

* *The floor of fourteen* is the maintainer's choice among the options put (14, 16, 18), taken with the second clause. Organic Law may set the age above it.
* *"exploits the immaturity or dependence".* Open to argument in every case; "dependence" reaches a teacher, a carer, a mentor. A jury judges it. `NARROWED`.
* *"a young person above that age".* No upper limit is stated; it ends where adulthood begins, which the canon does not define (the residual above stands).
* The other points of the red team above stand.

**Word count, second version:** current 28, draft 72, change +44.

## **Draft 8. Art. 5.4, new point 4** *(conflict 7, item 7b; SIM-QA-25; from SIM-QA-36 RED-12)* — **approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(wording approved; not merged; Chapter V to be recounted)*

**Current:** none. Art. 5.4 (parenting and alloparenting) has three points and names nobody who acts for a child in danger. The nearest guarantee is for an adult, Art. 8.5.3:
> * Emergency detention requires sortition validation (15 citizens) within seventy-two (72) hours, or release is mandatory;

**Draft:**
> 4. Anyone may take a child in danger to safety for the moment. Within seventy-two (72) hours a panel of three drawn by lot from outside the node hears the child and those who care for the child, decides where the child stays, and reviews its decision periodically.

**How the decision is carried:** "anyone", "for the moment"; three drawn from outside, within 72 hours; the child and the carers are heard; review.

**Placement.** Chapter V, amendable at 75%, and not Chapter I. Checked against Draft F (Art. 1.7.3, not merged): the carers exercise the child's rights "for the child's benefit", and "caring for and protecting a child is not holding under Article 1.2.5", so taking a child to safety is neither a holding nor a breach of the carers' role, and no further sentence is needed in Chapter I. *Residual:* a 75% majority could later remove this point; the rights of Draft F would remain.

**Red team of the wording.**
* *"in danger".* Not defined. A neighbour who disapproves of how a child is raised can act on it; the 72 hours and the panel are the guard, and three days is long for a small child.
* *No sentence on what happens if the panel does not sit in time.* Art. 8.5.3 says "or release is mandatory". Here the parallel would be "or the child returns", which may send a child back to danger. Left open on purpose; for the maintainer.
* *"periodically".* No period; Organic Law.
* *Who cares for the child during the 72 hours* is not said (the alloparenting hubs of point 2 are the obvious place); Organic Law.
* No one who works with children has read this.

**Word count:** draft 48 (new). Chapter V: canon 855, about 956 to 986 with accepted dockets, of 1,000: this draft may pass the ceiling; to be recounted.

## **Draft 9. Art. 8.2.1** *(conflict 3; SIM-QA-37 D-37.5)* — **approved by the maintainer 2026-11-20** *(wording approved as drafted, without the optional words on doors; the reading that a door or a barrier is a passive means is to be recorded at merge; not merged)*

**Current:**
> 1. Public safety is embedded in spatial architecture: corridors and dwellings maintain daylight sightlines, eliminating entrapment zones. Automated machinery contains optical interlocks triggering kinetic shutdowns upon human approach.

**Draft:**
> 1. Public safety is embedded in spatial architecture: corridors and dwellings maintain daylight sightlines, eliminating entrapment zones. Automated machinery contains optical interlocks triggering kinetic shutdowns upon human approach. No machine is built or used to seize or hold a person against their will.

**How the decision is carried:** one sentence. "Built or used" reaches a machine made for another purpose and turned to this one. "Seize or hold" names the act; shields, barriers and doors, which the decision allows, do neither.

**Red team of the wording.**
* *A door that locks.* A room whose door is locked by a mechanism holds a person. The custody of Art. 8.5 and the protective hold both need such a room. Read with the decision, a door is a passive means and the holding is done by the persons who decided it under Art. 1.2.5; the sentence could still be turned against any locked door. `NARROWED`: a reading to record at merge, or the words "a door or a barrier is not such a machine" (+10).
* *Care.* A lifting aid, a stretcher strap, a surgical robot and a vehicle's belt all hold a person, with consent. The draft therefore says "against their will".
* *The interdiction platforms of Art. 8.1.4* never target persons; no conflict.

**Word count:** current 28, draft 44, change +16.
