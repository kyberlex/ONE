## **II. FORENSIC PROOF-OF-IMMUNITY: THE FIVE RESOLVED VECTORS**

### **1. Vector ATK-001: Sortition Council Capture (Closed by Art. 4.2 & Art. 4.3.4)**
* **The Claim:** A coordinated cadre of 4 agents inside a 15-citizen council holds a 26.7% veto over supermajority decisions ($\ge 75\%$) and dominates lay citizens through pre-drafted dossiers.
* **Why the Exploit Fails in O.N.E.:**
  1. **Scale Hierarchy (Art. 4.2):** Neighborhood Sortition Councils (15 citizens, Art. 4.2.2) exercise jurisdiction strictly over localized municipal utility upkeep. Structural constitutional amendments require a 75% supermajority in the **Global Commons Assembly (1,001 citizens, Art. 4.2.5)** or **Bioregional Assemblies (101 citizens, Art. 4.2.3)**. In a 101 or 1,001 citizen assembly, 4 cadre members represent $0.4\%\text{--}3.9\%$, possessing zero blocking power.
  2. **Prohibition of Pre-Packaged Framing (Art. 4.3.4):** The Constitution explicitly makes it an *impeachable offense* for administrative staff or external groups to introduce pre-drafted dossiers or agenda filters. Agendas are generated directly by sortition delegates under mandatory anti-entrenchment rotation (Art. 4.3.3).

### **CHALLENGE OP-01: Sensor Telemetry Salami-Drift & Analog Field Ground-Truthing**
* **Originating Vector:** Ex-Attack 003 (Oracle Telemetry)
* **Domain:** Environmental Telemetry & PLC Security
* **Target Articles:** [`Art. 3.4.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md), [`Art. 3.4.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md), [`Art. 7.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_07.md)

#### **The Empirical Friction:**
Even with open-source PLCs and deterministic firmware, remote physical sensors (piezometric well meters, acoustic stream gauges, soil nitrogen sensors) can suffer hardware calibration drift or localized physical fouling that statistical anomaly detectors may classify as natural seasonal variance ($\Delta \le 0.3\sigma$).

#### **The Operational Solution (Field Protocol):**
1. **Zero-Knowledge Multi-Party Telemetry:** Sensor firmware signs raw measurements within secure hardware enclaves (TEE) before submitting data to decentralized mesh telemetry.
2. **Citizen Analog Ground-Truthing Squads:** Neighborhood Sortition Councils regularly commission lay citizen teams equipped with manual, analog tools (physical dipsticks, titration reagents, mechanical flow meters) to perform unpredictable spot-checks against digital dashboard readouts, ensuring cybernetic systems remain firmly tethered to physical reality.

### **CHALLENGE OP-02: Cryptographic Anti-Brokerage for High-Demand FabLab Queues**
* **Originating Vector:** Ex-Attack 004 (Inelastic Booking Queues)
* **Domain:** Property Usufruct & Tier 3 Open Fab-Labs
* **Target Articles:** [`Art. 2.1.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md), [`Art. 2.2.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md), [`Art. 3.3.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)

#### **The Empirical Friction:**
While real estate and productive land are fully protected under dynamic usufruct (Art. 2.1/2.5), high-demand specialized machinery (e.g., 5-axis CNC mills, metal 3D printers, semiconductor cleanroom hours) can experience physical booking queues. An informal favor market could theoretically emerge if users book machine slots speculatively and trade their reservation slots for personal gifts.

#### **The Operational Solution (Protocol Design):**
1. **Soulbound Cryptographic Reservation Tokens:** FabLab scheduling systems utilize non-transferable, identity-bound booking tokens. A reservation slot cannot be re-assigned, transferred, or swapped.
2. **Dynamic Slot Forfeiture:** If the registered custodian is not physically present at the workstation within 15 minutes of the scheduled start time, the slot automatically forfeits to the next citizen drawn by lot from the standby queue.
