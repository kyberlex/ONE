### 1.1 Mathematical Formulation of the Exploit
Let the population be $N$. Essential Class-A maintenance (e.g., manual sewer unclogging, toxic metallurgical recycling, high-voltage maintenance, intensive non-chemical agricultural weeding) imposes an acute somatic disutility (labor cost) $C_{\text{labor}} > 0$.

2. Elective discretionary surplus $\epsilon_{\text{Tier3}}$ (fab-lab machine time, high-latency travel credits, specialized tools) accessible only via reciprocity compliance (Article 3.3.3, Article 5.2.2).

The net utility functions for an individual $i$ under cooperation ($C$) and defection ($D$) are:
$$\mathcal{U}_i(C) = B_{\text{Tier1}} - C_{\text{labor}} + \epsilon_{\text{Tier3}}$$
$$\mathcal{U}_i(D) = B_{\text{Tier1}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{leisure}}$$
where $\mathcal{L}_{\text{leisure}} \ge 0$ is the sovereign utility of total temporal autonomy, personal pursuits, and exemption from hazardous somatic strain.

For Class-A essential maintenance, the somatic revulsion, physical risk, and physiological wear satisfy:
$$C_{\text{labor}} > \epsilon_{\text{Tier3}} + \mathcal{L}_{\text{leisure}} \implies \mathcal{U}_i(D) > \mathcal{U}_i(C)$$

Because $B_{\text{Tier1}}$ is constitutionally invariant and guaranteed *in perpetuum*, defection dominates cooperation. Under standard replicator dynamics:
$$\dot{x} = x(1 - x)[\mathcal{U}(C) - \mathcal{U}(D)]$$
where $x$ is the share of cooperators. Since $[\mathcal{U}(C) - \mathcal{U}(D)] < 0$, $\dot{x} < 0$ for all $x \in (0, 1]$. **The unique Evolutionarily Stable Strategy (ESS) is $x^* = 0$ (total systemic defection).**
