# **Restore and Elaborate** `[STATUS: SIMULATION_HYPOTHESIS]`

*Register of the plain-language pass (board instruction 11, [`SIM_QA_TODO.md`](SIM_QA_TODO.md)). Started 2026-11-13.*

Nothing is removed from the Constitution because it is vague or has no stated consequence. Such a phrase stays in the text and is recorded here with its location, what is missing (a definition, a consequence, who decides) and a one-line proposal. Every place where two honest readers could reach different results is recorded here too, unresolved. Each entry is a future docket; none is decided by being listed. Proposals are starting points for that docket, not text.

**Kinds:** *Vague* (a word or phrase without a definition), *No consequence* (a rule or statement with nothing that follows from it), *Who decides* (a judgment with no body named), *Two readings* (two honest readers reach different results).
