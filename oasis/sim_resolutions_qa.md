## **SIM-QA-01: Quorum Scaling & Demarchic Viability for Emerging Nodes (< 50 Pop)**

* **Date Recorded:** 2026-09-22  
* **Date Merged:** 2026-10-05  
* **Epistemic Status:** `[STATUS: CANONICAL_MERGED]` (Integrated into Living Constitution Arts. 4.2.1, 4.2.2)  
* **Originating Settlement:** Detroit Delray Commons (Starting Pop: 28)  
* **Constitutional Articles:** [`Art. 4.2.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md), [`Art. 4.2.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md), [`Art. 4.5`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md), [`Art. 5.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_05.md)

### **1. The Material / Empirical Friction**
In the simulation engine, a founding micro-commons node initializes with a modest pioneer population ($N = 28$ citizens). Under a naive interpretation of Chapter IV, convening the **Neighborhood Sortition Council of 15 citizens** ([`Art. 4.2.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md)) requisitions **53.6% of the adult population** into deliberative assembly duty.

Because assembly duty mandates total exemption from routine physical labor ([`Art. 4.3.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md)), the remaining 13 citizens are mathematically incapable of completing the mandatory daily *chore roster* (8h greenhouse farming, 6h water/pumps, 6h microgrid maintenance, 8h fablab recycling). The node rapidly incurs a severe labor deficit, precipitating greywater overflow and food supply collapse within 12 simulated days.

Conversely, abolishing sortition or granting executive decree power to a single administrator would violate the Class-0 Invariant of Demarchy ([`Art. 4.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md)).

### **2. The Tested Countermeasure (Subsidiarity Scale)**
The simulation implements the **Principle of Demarchic Subsidiarity Scale**:
1. **Emerging Node (Population $< 50$):**
   - Sovereign local deliberation is assigned to the **Local Mediation Panel (3 citizens drawn by lot)** pursuant to [`Art. 4.2.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md).
   - Strict odd-number parity is preserved (preventing ties), while sequestering only ~10% of the active workforce.
2. **Consolidated Neighborhood (Population $\ge 50$):**
   - Automatically transitions to the full **Neighborhood Sortition Council (15 citizens)** pursuant to [`Art. 4.2.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md).
3. **Graduated Decision Thresholds ([`Art. 4.5`](../bible/chapters/chapter_04.md)):**
   - **60% Qualified Majority:** 2 of 3 votes (Tier 1) / 9 of 15 votes (Tier 2) for routine allocations.
   - **75% Constitutional Supermajority:** 3 of 3 votes (Tier 1) / 12 of 15 votes (Tier 2) for asylum, structural revisions, or emergency covenants.
4. **Cognitive & Screen Accessibility Invariant:**
   - Instead of 15 individual citizen cards cluttering mobile displays, deliberation stances are synthesized via a compact vector **Solarpunk Donut Chart (< 90px)** with a visual threshold notch at 60%/75%, accompanied by an expandable drawer for inspecting juror attributes on demand.

### **3. Telemetric Validation**
- 100-day headless deterministic simulation run with $N = 28$:
  - Zero labor deficit hours.
  - Closed thermodynamic balance ($L_{min}$ floor sustained at 100%).
  - Demarchic rotation executed every 30 days (`720 ticks`) without oligarchy or consecutive terms.

### **4. Open Question for Future Ratification**
*Should the population boundary of 50 residents be formally codified as a statutory annotation to Article 4.2 in the Living Constitution, or should nodes have autonomy to calibrate this transition between 40 and 60 residents based on local automation levels?*

## **SIM-QA-02: Dual-Tier Tool Boundary: Civic Tool Libraries vs. Inviolable Personal Craft Gear**

* **Date Recorded:** 2026-09-23  
* **Date Merged:** 2026-10-05  
* **Epistemic Status:** `[STATUS: CANONICAL_MERGED]` (Integrated into Living Constitution Art. 2.2.1)  
* **Originating Context:** Urban apartment block & low-capital seed node field dynamics (Tbilisi/Georgia working-class housing dialogue)  
* **Constitutional Articles:** [`Art. 2.2.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md), [`Art. 2.2.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md), [`Art. 3.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)

### **1. The Material / Empirical Friction**
In ground-level seed nodes (e.g., dense 2–3 story urban apartment blocks with limited capital and zero immediate budget for rooftop solar or high-tech fablabs), an acute operational tension arises between two core tenets of O.N.E.:
1. **The Inviolability of Personal Effects & Craft Gear ([`Art. 2.2.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md)):** Citizens must feel somatic and psychological security that their personal instruments, digital workstations, and artisanal trade tools cannot be collectivized or requisitioned by neighborhood fiat.
2. **The Anti-Waste & De-financialization Imperative:** In a typical 20-household building, 20 separate families each privately purchase cheap, redundant utility tools (e.g., hammer drills, extension ladders, angle grinders, sewing machines, automotive jacks). An electric rotary drill has an empirical average utilization of only **12 to 15 minutes across its entire operating lifespan**, representing dead capital extraction by retail monopolies and unnecessary landfill waste.

If a seed node demands total collectivization of all tools, skilled craftspeople and artisans refuse to participate (fearing that their precision, calibrated, or sentimentally vital tools will be damaged by careless neighbors). If the node remains entirely hyper-individualized, poor working-class residents remain trapped in debt and unable to maintain their building.

### **2. The Tested Operational Countermeasure (Dual-Tier Tool Classification)**
The node operating protocol establishes an explicit, objective boundary between **Personal Vocation Gear** and **Civic Utility Libraries**:

1. **Tier 1 — Inviolable Personal Craft Gear ([`Art. 2.2.1`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md)):**
   - Defined as tools of high daily utilization, calibrated precision, intimate creative expression, or personal trade specialization (e.g., luthier chisels, developer laptops, surgeon/dentist implements, artist brushes, chef knives, personalized mechanic torque sets).
   - **Juridical Status:** Inviolable individual possession. They can never be expropriated, borrowed without express bilateral consent, or pooled into communal circulation.

2. **Tier 2 — Civic Tool Library & Maintenance Pool ([`Art. 2.2.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_02.md)):**
   - Defined as intermittent, low-frequency, high-durability infrastructure maintenance equipment (e.g., rotary masonry drills, telescoping aluminum ladders, drain augers, high-pressure washers, heavy sewing/canvas machines, lawn aerators).
   - **Juridical Status:** Held under communal usufruct in a common entrance locker or basement nook. Governed by a simple physical/mesh checkout log.
   - **Maintenance & Sharpening:** Maintained, lubricated, and serviced as part of the node’s shared chore hours or circular fablab recycling roster.

3. **The Voluntary Custody Registry ("Adopt-a-Tool"):**
   - Residents who privately own Tier 2 tools may deposit them into the Civic Tool Library voluntarily.
   - **Incentive / Reciprocity:** The community assumes 100% of the replacement/maintenance cost (sharpening drill bits, replacing batteries) through collective reserves, while the depositor retains unconditional priority reservation whenever they require the tool.

### **3. Simulation & Field Validation**
- Modeled in building-scale metabolic runs ($N = 15–30$ households):
  - Achieves a **~75% reduction in household hardware expenditures** during the founding year.
  - Zero reports of craft expropriation disputes or artisan alienation.
  - Serves as the primary behavioral bridge for building social trust ("metabolic muscle memory") prior to implementing higher-tier solar or water microgrids.

### **4. Open Question for Future Ratification**
*Should the boundary between Tier 1 (Inviolable Craft) and Tier 2 (Civic Library) be governed by an objective checklist of asset classes in statutory annexes, or left to rapid subsidiarity determination by the Local Mediation Panel (Art. 4.2.1) upon dispute?*

### **2. Adversarial Stress-Test (RED-5 Assault)**

### **3. The Hardened Operational Countermeasure (Purple Patch)**
1. **Strict Open Hardware Mandate ([`Art. 3.4.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)):** Prohibition of closed proprietary binary blobs or untrusted enclaves; all telemetry hardware must run fully verifiable open firmware.
2. **Citizen Ground-Truthing Squads ([`Art. 3.4.5`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)):** Lay citizen teams equipped with manual analog tools (calibrated dipsticks, mechanical flow meters, titration reagents) conduct unpredictable spot-checks against digital readouts.

### **1. The Material / Empirical Friction**
While real estate, land, and housing are perpetually de-commodified under dynamic usufruct (Art. 2.1 and Art. 2.5), localized physical bottlenecks can occur for high-demand specialized machinery in Tier 3 Open Fab-Labs (e.g., 5-axis CNC mills, metal laser sintering, cleanroom lithography). If reservation queues are open and fungible, an informal secondary market could theoretically emerge where individuals reserve prime tool-hours speculatively and trade access to third parties in exchange for personal gifts or informal favors.

### **2. Adversarial Stress-Test (RED-1 Assault)**
* **Vector 1 (In-Person Proxy Fabrication):** If reservations are soulbound, a scalper simply attends in person, scans their own badge, and executes a third-party client's proprietary G-code file in exchange for unrecorded cash or favors.
* **Vector 2 (Sybil Queue Squatting):** A colluding group reserves prime production blocks under automated accounts with dummy jobs, running zero-spindle dry air cuts or cancelling at the 14-minute mark to block competitors.
* **Vector 3 (Parasitic Commercial Enclosure):** An entrepreneur uses free communal tool time, grid power, and tooling wear to manufacture closed-source proprietary goods for external monetary sale.

### **3. The Hardened Operational Countermeasure (Purple Patch)**
1. **The Execution-Equals-Publication Invariant ([`Art. 1.4`](../bible/chapters/chapter_01.md) & [`Art. 3.3.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)):** Any job executed on communal Tier 3 machinery must commit its toolpaths, G-code, and CAM bills of materials to the public local mesh repository. Secret, proprietary, or DRM-locked manufacturing on communal tooling is unconstitutional.
2. **Quadratic Frequency Dampening ([`Art. 3.3.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_03.md)):** Individual queue priority decays quadratically based on trailing 30-day cumulative machine utilization hours ($W = 1 / (1 + \sum h^2)$), ensuring occasional makers and new creators are never locked out by intensive operators.
3. **Soulbound Booking & Dynamic 15-Minute Forfeiture:** Booking tokens are non-transferable; unattended workstations forfeit after 15 minutes to standby creators drawn by lot, with repeat unexcused no-shows incurring temporary peak-slot booking cooldowns.

## **SIM-QA-05: Codification of Civic Rescue, Firefighting & Disaster Response Guilds**

* **Date Recorded:** 2026-10-05  
* **Date Merged:** 2026-10-05  
* **Epistemic Status:** `[STATUS: CANONICAL_MERGED]` (Integrated into Living Constitution Arts. 5.1.3, 8.1.2)  
* **Originating Context:** Habitat Resiliency Modeling & Operational Analysis of Chapter VIII (Public Safety vs. Physical Entropy Asymmetry)  
* **Constitutional Articles:** [`Art. 5.1.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_05.md), [`Art. 8.1.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_08.md), [`Art. 8.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_08.md), [`Art. 10.4.4`](../bible/chapters/chapter_10.md)

### **1. The Material / Empirical Friction**
In the Living Constitution, public safety is formulated almost exclusively around the abolition of militarized policing, interpersonal dispute de-escalation, restorative justice circles, and non-carceral custody. Article 8.1.2 creates *"unarmed Civic Peace Guilds trained in de-escalation and trauma triage"*.

However, physical reality and thermodynamic entropy generate acute hazards independent of human malicious intent or social friction:
1. **Physical and Combustion Disasters:** Conflagrations in timber/strawbale modular habitat units (MHU), high-voltage battery energy storage system (BESS) thermal runaways, and catastrophic forest/wildland fires.
2. **Geophysical and Meteorological Shocks:** Flash flooding, riverbank levee breaches, structural earthquake entrapments, alpine/speleological rescues, and industrial hazardous material (Hazmat) neutralizations.

Conflating technical rescue and firefighting with "Peace Guilds" or "interpersonal mediation" creates a lethal operational category error:
* Peace Guild mediators are trained in somatic trauma and dialogue, not in high-angle rope extraction, breathing apparatus (SCBA) protocols, hydraulic extrication, or dynamic wildfire flanking.
* Unlike armed policing (which historically protects absentee property monopolies and sovereign borders through coercive violence), **firefighting and search-and-rescue (SAR) represent the purest historical embodiment of non-carceral mutual aid**.
* Failing to explicitly codify a dedicated, technical **Civic Rescue & Firefighting Guild** leaves ambiguous how emergency command functions during sub-minute life-and-death crises, how equipment is provisioned, and how training/readiness hours are credited under the labor reciprocity matrix.

### **2. The Tested Operational Countermeasure (Institutional Bifurcation & ICS)**
The operational simulation implements a deterministic bifurcation of public safety and emergency functions:

1. **Explicit Domain Bifurcation:**
   * **Civic Peace Guilds ([`Art. 8.1.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_08.md)):** Stewards of human social peace, interpersonal restorative justice, de-escalation, and psychiatric/trauma triage.
   * **Civic Rescue & Fire Guilds / Civil Protection Brigades ([`Art. 8.1.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_08.md) & [`Art. 10.4.4`](../bible/chapters/chapter_10.md)):** Autonomous technical guilds specialized in structural and wildland firefighting, technical search and rescue (SAR), flood/seismic disaster response, and chemical/biological hazard mitigation.

2. **De-Militarized Incident Command Protocol (Open ICS):**
   * During active kinetic/environmental crises (active fires, structural collapses, hazardous plumes), tactical operational authority is temporarily vested in certified **Incident Technical Coordinators** drawn from the Rescue Guild, operating under flat, open Incident Command System protocols.
   * Decisions are strictly bounded to life-safety, victim triage, and structural containment; coordinators possess zero judicial, carceral, or property-disposition authority.

3. **Hybrid Staffing & Peak Ergonomic Multipliers ([`Art. 5.1.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_05.md)):**
   * **Core Technical Mentors:** A standing cadre of certified master instructors and technical specialists who maintain complex apparatus (pumps, thermal drones, breathing gear, cutting torches).
   * **Rotational & Voluntary Reserves:** Civic cohorts drawn from the local population completing rotational shifts and voluntary on-call standby.
   * **Labor Reciprocity:** Active emergency deployments, technical training drills, and hazardous standby carry peak **ergonomic time multipliers of $4\times$ to $5\times$** against baseline chore obligations pursuant to Article 5.1.3.

4. **Hardware Standard & Analog Resilience ([`Art. 6.1.3`](../bible/chapters/chapter_06.md) & [`Art. 6.2.2`](../bible/chapters/chapter_06.md)):**
   * Firefighting and rescue apparatus are standardized across open-hardware blueprints: electric/hybrid high-torque tender chassis, pressurized gravity-fed cistern hydrants (requiring zero grid power), and thermal imaging drones operating ephemeral, zero-biometric edge telemetry.

5. **Inter-Watershed Mutual Aid Logistics ([`Art. 10.4.4`](../bible/chapters/chapter_10.md)):**
   * Large-scale catastrophes (e.g., wildland conflagrations exceeding local suppression capacity) trigger automated inter-basin mobilization corridors, transferring specialized equipment and mobile strike brigades across adjacent watersheds without political delay.

## **SIM-QA-06: Governance, Thermodynamic Calibration & Usufruct Transformation of Legacy High-Capital Assets (Fabs, Data Centers & Megacities)**

* **Date Merged:** 2026-10-21  
* **Epistemic Status:** `[STATUS: CANONICAL_MERGED]` (Integrated into Living Constitution Arts. 2.2, 3.3.4, 6.2, 7.4, 10.3)

* **Master Architectural Specification:** [`oasis/legacy_high_capital_assets.md`](legacy_high_capital_assets.md)

### **1. The Material / Empirical Friction**
In standard post-capitalist discourse, alternative systems either regress into agrarian parochialism (dismantling advanced industry) or preserve corporate/state technocracies. Advanced semiconductor fabrication (EUV lithography at 3nm/2nm), chlor-alkali and petrochemical synthesis, hyperscale data centers, and multi-million-citizen metropolises cannot be atomized into small neighborhood workshops without catastrophic yield loss, thermodynamic failure, and somatic collapse.

### **2. The Tested Operational Countermeasures (The Five Mechanisms)**
1. **Classification as Tier 2-A "Indivisible Capital Goods" (Art. 2.2 & Art. 3.3.4):**
   - High-throughput facilities are recognized as indivisible social capital under Federated Commons Usufruct.
   - Technical worker guilds manage operations under the democratic oversight of Bioregional (101 delegates) and Global (1,001 delegates) Citizen Assemblies.
   - Physical capital depreciation reserves (tool wear, catalyst degradation, target replacement) must be fully replenished from the thermodynamic ledger before surplus is released to Tier 3 discretionary fab-labs.
2. **Thermodynamic Mass-Balance Re-Engineering (Art. 3.1 & Art. 3.4):**
   - Advanced fabs must implement closed-circuit evaporation, catalytic chemical recovery, and dry-scrubbing. Breaches of river basin regenerative boundaries trigger the Thermodynamic Lexicographic Viability Operator ($\mathcal{L}_{\min}$) to deterministically throttle synthesis run-lengths and protect somatic water baselines.
   - Hyperscale data centers and exothermic synthesis pipe reject heat directly into district heating, greenhouse biomass loops, or low-temperature organic Rankine cycle turbines.
3. **Compute Infrastructure to Federated Commons (Ch. VII & Art. 6.4):**

* **A:**

  * New Art. 4.5.4 (Threshold Arithmetic): the smallest whole number of votes reaching the share of *seated* members.

* However, a fully mobilized 20% faction still takes about 50% of agenda slots. The defense converts exhaustion into a capped, roughly proportional tax on agenda time; it does not eliminate it.
* The defense depends on Zero-Knowledge Proof-of-Personhood holding at scale, which is unproven.
* **Separate docket recommended:** in 3-person panels, a single hostile panelist can force a plebiscite on every 75% matter. The probability that at least one faction member is seated is 0.14 / 0.27 / 0.49 for factions of 5 / 10 / 20%.
