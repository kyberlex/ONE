O.N.E. assumes in Article 5.4 that universal alloparenting and communal nurseries can replace or subordinate lineage-based parental investment. This conflicts with evolutionary biology:
* **Hamilton’s Rule ($rB > C$):** Natural selection systematically favors resource allocation and physiological protection directed toward biological kin ($r = 0.5$ for offspring, $0.25$ for grandchildren/siblings) over unrelated conspecifics ($r \approx 0$).
* When Article 5.4 attempts to mandate that young adults rotate through communal nurseries, traditionalist clans face an evolutionary cost: their reproductive effort and energetic investment are siphoned off into genetic competitors.
* Resistance to communal nurseries is not an ideological deviation that can be eradicated via "epistemic hygiene" (Art. 5.3.2); it is an evolved behavioral adaptation. Clans will covertly withdraw their children from communal centers, declaring home sanctuary under Article 1.2 (Bodily Sovereignty) and Article 2.2.2 (Possessory Usufruct of the Family Homestead).

#### 2. Behavioral Game Theory: The Impotence of Non-Carceral Sanctions
In standard behavioral economics, prosocial cooperation in large-scale groups ($N > 150$) requires effective, credible punishment mechanisms ("altruistic punishment"). In O.N.E., the constitutional architecture deliberately strips society of every coercive enforcement tool:
* No kinetic force (Art. 5.2.2);
* No carceral detention (Art. 8.5.1);
* No reduction of survival calories, water, or medical access (Art. 1.3.3 & Art. 5.2.1);
* No somatic displacement or involuntary eviction from shelter (Art. 2.3.1 & Art. 5.2.2).

The terminal sanction—**Metabolic Decoupling**—merely confines the defector to their existing footprint with guaranteed Tier 1 life support. In game-theoretic terms, the "punishment" leaves the defector's baseline fitness intact while setting their civic contribution costs to zero. Because defection carries zero fitness penalty, the strategy spreads culturally and behaviorally through imitation.

#### 3. Cybernetic and Thermodynamic Breakdown of Article 5.1.3
The safety valve specified in Article 5.1.3 is cybernetically unstable:
* To prevent coercion, unstaffed nodes are throttled or decommissioned until open robotics can automate them.
* However, developing, manufacturing, and deploying open robotics requires high-entropy capital goods, mineral processing, cleanroom operations, and precision maintenance—all of which rely on upstream physical labor.
* When essential agricultural and sanitation lines are decommissioned due to labor shortages, the thermodynamic carrying capacity drops. The system is forced to allocate its scarce remaining energy and machine time strictly to somatic survival (via the Thermodynamic Lexicographic Viability Operator, Art. 3.1.5).
* Discretionary engineering and automation rollouts are frozen. The automation promised to replace the missing labor cannot materialize because the labor required to build the automation has evaporated.

Why can sortition assemblies not correct this loophole?
