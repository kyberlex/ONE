### A. Evolutionary Psychology & Kin Selection Failures
O.N.E. relies on a model of post-tribal species-wide altruism that directly violates the foundational laws of human behavioral ecology.

1. **Hamilton’s Rule ($rB > C$):** Natural selection favors altruistic behavior only when the genetic relatedness ($r$) between the actor and recipient multiplied by the reproductive benefit ($B$) to the recipient exceeds the somatic cost ($C$) to the actor. 
   * In O.N.E.’s rotational labor pool (Art. 5.1), an individual is required to expend high somatic energy ($C_{\text{labor}}$) clearing human feces, handling toxic industrial swarf, and tilling soil for an undifferentiated municipal commons where average relatedness to the recipients approaches zero ($r \to 0$).
   * Conversely, investing time inside the clan enclave to rear biological children and support kin boasts an evolutionary relatedness of $r = 0.5$ (offspring, siblings) and $r = 0.25$ (grandchildren, nieces/nephews).
   * Consequently, $rB < C$ for all communal maintenance labor, whereas $rB > C$ holds massively for domestic kin investment. The human organism is neurologically hardwired to defect from the municipal commons and prioritize the lineage.
2. **Trivers’ Parental Investment Theory vs. Communal Alloparenting:** Parental investment is costly, asymmetrical, and fiercely defended. Throughout human evolutionary history, ceding child care and ideological conditioning to unrelated conspecifics exposes offspring to exploitation, neglect, or cultural erasure. Article 5.4’s universal communal alloparenting assumes humans will treat unrelated offspring with the same biological devotion as their own genetic lineage. In reality, parental vigilance triggers xenophobic boundary formation, suspicion of unrelated caretakers, and violent resistance to state-enforced pediatric separation.
3. **Destruction of Altruistic Punishment:** Extensive empirical research across experimental economics (e.g., Fehr & Gächter; Bowles & Gintis) demonstrates that public goods games avoid collapse *only* when participants possess the capacity for **altruistic punishment**—the ability of group members to impose direct, personally costly, and physically meaningful sanctions on free-riders. Article 1.3.3 permanently outlaws this mechanism: *"Physical survival shall never be leveraged by any assembly or collective as an instrument of behavioral conditioning, labor extraction, or social punishment."* By stripping the cooperative majority of the power to materially discipline defectors (no caloric docking, no physical eviction, no incarceration), O.N.E. condemns cooperators to the **Sucker’s Payoff**.

### B. Game-Theoretic & Public Goods Disintegration
The interaction between labor contribution and consumption in O.N.E. constitutes an $N$-player Prisoner's Dilemma with an unconditional safety net.

1. **Payoff Dominance of Defection:**
   Let the payoff of an individual $i$ be:
   $$\Pi_i = B(\text{Tier 1}) + \beta \cdot B(\text{Tier 3}) - C(e_i)$$
   where $e_i$ is physical maintenance labor exertion, $C(e_i)$ is the somatic effort cost, $B(\text{Tier 1})$ is the invariant survival guarantee, and $\beta \in [0, 1]$ is the preference weighting for Tier 3 surplus.
   * If citizen $i$ cooperates ($e_i = e_{\text{mandate}}$), they bear the somatic cost $C(e_{\text{mandate}})$, receive $B(\text{Tier 1})$, and gain access to Tier 3 ($\beta B(\text{Tier 3})$).
   * If citizen $i$ defects ($e_i = 0$), under Article 5.2 they lose Tier 3 ($\beta \cdot 0 = 0$), but their $B(\text{Tier 1})$ is structurally invariant and guaranteed for life. Their somatic cost is zero ($C(0) = 0$).
   * Whenever $C(e_{\text{mandate}}) > \beta \cdot B(\text{Tier 3})$, defection is the dominant individual strategy.

The marginal utility of discretionary fab-lab access or long-distance train credits cannot compensate for somatic exhaustion, exposure to pathogens, and musculoskeletal degradation.

The perceived unfairness triggers immediate retaliatory defection. The rotational roster experiences an exponential cascade of absenteeism.
