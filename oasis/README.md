# **O-ASIS: EPISTEMIC ADVERSARIAL STRESS-TEST ENGINE**
## *Simulating Failure Modes, Constitutional Fuzzing & Thermodynamic Resilience*

Welcome to the **O-ASIS Research & Red-Teaming Track** of **Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.)**.

O-ASIS is the dedicated adversarial proving ground for the **Living Constitution of O.N.E.** ([`ONE NETWORKED EARTH (O.N.E.).md`](../ONE%20NETWORKED%20EARTH%20(O.N.E.).md)). Rather than treating constitutional design as a static ideological doctrine, O-ASIS subjects every article, flow balance, and demarchic rule to systematic adversarial pressure testing (Game Theory, Leontief Thermodynamics, Mechanism Design, and Deliberative Demarchy).
