### 1. EXPLOIT MECHANISM & PHYSICAL DEADLOCK

#### A. The Transient Hydraulic Degradation Loop
Under Article 6.1.3, the system mitigates low-resource operational anomalies via *batch-cycled timed head retention* rather than continuous aperture throttling to prevent Darcy-Weisbach siphon breaks at high elevations. However, in an extended Black-Sky regime (Day 30+):

1. **Pressure Transients ($Joukowsky\ Shock$):** Manual and analog-mechanical valve actuation induces rapid rate-of-flow changes ($\frac{dv}{dt}$). The resulting transient pressure spike is governed by the Joukowsky equation:
   $$\Delta P = \rho \cdot c \cdot \Delta v$$
   Where $\rho \approx 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$, acoustic wave speed in water-filled rigid conduits $c \approx 1000\text{–}1400\text{ m/s}$, and $\Delta v$ is the instantaneous velocity step. In the absence of sub-second electronic closed-loop modulation, mechanical damping relies solely on passive hydraulic accumulators.
2. **Seal Degradation & Cavitation:** As accumulators reach thermal and mechanical hysteresis limits under recurring batch cycles ($N > 10^4$ cycles across 30 days), mechanical valves suffer seat chatter and cavitation. The pressure drop across eroding valve orifices drops below the vapor pressure of water ($P_v \approx 2.34\text{ kPa}$ at $20^\circ\text{C}$), generating localized vapor bubble collapse that pits metal seats and strips elastomer gaskets.
3. **Elastomer Stockout:** Specialized synthetic elastomeric seals (e.g., EPDM, Viton/FKM, nitrile butadiene) undergo irreversible mechanical compression set and extrusion failure under cyclic shock loading. Because high-durometer vulcanized elastomers cannot be synthesized via ambient additive manufacturing (Article 6.3.2), the system faces an unresolvable physical boundary: leaking valve seals cannot hold head retention during closed intervals.

#### B. The Downstream Hydraulic Siphon Break

* Static head in stepped break-pressure tanks cannot recover during inter-batch recharge windows.
* The hydraulic grade line (HGL) drops below the physical invert of elevated distribution branches.

* **Deadlock:** Water cannot physically reach elevated sectors by gravity alone, regardless of the constitutional survival entitlement codified in Chapter I.
