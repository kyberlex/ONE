## **Part A: Consolidated Text (every accepted patch)**

#### **CHAPTER IV: GOVERNANCE BY LOT: THE SORTITION ARCHITECTURE**

##### **Article 4.1 — The Principle of the Lot (Demarchy) and Abolition of Oligarchy**

1. All sovereign legislative, deliberative, and constitutional authority emanates exclusively from assemblies of citizens chosen by stratified random lottery (sortition), and from the assemblies of all adult residents of Article 4.2.1.

2. Electoral party politics, campaign financing, corporate lobbying, and permanent parliamentary classes are abolished as vectors of oligarchic capture.

3. Citizens remain free to associate and advocate (Article 1.4), but no association may finance, nominate, instruct, or reward sortition delegates; any such act is void and constitutes a structural assault on the civic commons.

##### **Article 4.2 — Tiered Assembly Architecture and Invariant Odd Parity**

Deliberative authority is structured through concentric citizen chambers matched to bioregional scales, each in strict odd-number parity to prevent ties and deadlock:

1. **Local Mediation Panels:** Three (3) citizens, drawn for each dispute and dissolved when they rule, for neighborhood conflicts, domestic safety, and housing restitution.

   * *Nodes Below the Council Threshold:* A node of fewer than one hundred and fifty (150) adult residents holds no drawn governing body. Matters internal to the node (work rosters, water, food growing, the local energy grid, allocation of free dwellings, settling of newcomers admitted under Article 10.4), within the limits fixed by Organic Law, are decided by the assembly of all adult residents, voting by secret ballot at the request of any one of them and always on dwellings, newcomers and disputes, under a chair drawn by lot for each session; as an exception to Article 4.5.4, thresholds in this assembly are counted on votes cast, with the quorum fixed by Organic Law. A rule of the larger node on these matters does not bind the node. All other matters belong to the Neighborhood Sortition Council of the nearest larger node of the same bioregion, whose draw includes the node's adult residents and which hears the node's assembly before deciding a matter that concerns the node specifically. Disputes between residents are heard by a Local Mediation Panel of three (3) citizens drawn for each dispute under the rule for outside panels of Article 4.2. A dispute over whether a matter is internal is heard by a panel of three (3) citizens drawn under the same rule, so from neither the node nor the larger node nor any node in its draw pool, with one appeal (*Provocatio*) to a cross-watershed blind panel of fifteen (15). The node holds a right of appeal (*Provocatio*) to a cross-watershed blind panel of fifteen (15) citizens against any decision of that Council that concerns the node alone. Until a larger node exists, the assembly alone governs, and the mediation panel is drawn from the nearest other nodes or, if none exists, from the node itself with no first-degree kin or domestic partners serving together.

2. **Neighborhood Sortition Councils:** Fifteen (15) citizens, serving a mandate whose length is drawn within the range fixed by Organic Law for that tier, overseeing local commons, utilities, and facilities (constituted upon a node sustaining one hundred and fifty (150) adult residents for twelve months, and dissolved into the assembly of Article 4.2.1 only upon falling below one hundred and twenty (120) for twelve months).

3. **Bioregional Citizen Assemblies:** One hundred and one (101) delegates, serving a mandate whose length is drawn within the range fixed by Organic Law for that tier, governing watershed ecological balances, industrial zoning, and inter-node agreements.

4. **Continental Governance Chambers:** Three hundred and one (301) citizens, serving a mandate whose length is drawn within the range fixed by Organic Law for that tier, coordinating continental backbones, regeneration corridors, and macro-balances.

5. **Global Commons Assembly:** One thousand and one (1,001) citizens, serving a mandate whose length is drawn within the range fixed by Organic Law for that tier, setting planetary thermodynamic boundaries and ratifying cross-continental covenants.

The public draw that selects a member also draws the length of the mandate. The length is sealed at the draw by a commitment that anyone can verify once revealed, and is disclosed to the member and to the body at the same time, at a notice fixed by Organic Law (no less than one month, and no less than three months where service is at least half of a full working week).

Panels of three (3) and councils of fifteen (15) are juries, impartial because drawn, not statistical samples of opinion. Statistical representativeness is claimed only for bodies of one hundred and one (101) or more, and every such claim states its margin of error.

Panels of three (3) that mediate a dispute, classify a matter or certify a decision are drawn for that dispute or request and dissolved when they rule; they hold no mandate. Where this Constitution or Organic Law requires a panel drawn from outside, it is drawn from nodes that are neither a party to the matter nor in the draw pool of a party. After ruling, each member observes the hiatus of Article 4.3.3, counted on the length of service.

##### **Article 4.3 — Stratified Selection, Universal Service, and Anti-Bureaucratic Rotation**

1. Jurors for all sortition bodies are selected from universal civic registers through open, publicly verifiable cryptographic and mechanical lotteries. Stratification mirrors the population across age, gender, and geography. Each draw produces ordered alternates within every stratum; a seat left by withdrawal, deferral or excusal passes to the next alternate of the same stratum. Refusal, withdrawal and excusal rates, including counts by excusal ground, are published per stratum without identifying persons; a rate well above the others triggers review of service conditions, never of persons.

2. Civic service by lot is a universal prerogative and a civic duty to answer the summons. A summoned citizen attends an orientation session at a first mandate and may then withdraw without penalty, stigma, or loss of any right (Article 5.2.3). Each citizen may defer one summons without stating a reason; excusal on grounds listed in Organic Law is self-certified and never judged on merit. Service occupies a share of a full working week fixed by Organic Law for each tier; citizens who serve receive release from routine labor in proportion to the hours served, and replacement of domestic and care support for those hours, arranged before service begins. Local bodies meet in person. Higher tiers may deliberate remotely, with sessions in person at the ratio fixed by Organic Law; at every tier, decisions whose substance requires sixty percent (60%) or seventy-five percent (75%) are deliberated and voted only at sessions in person; the procedural votes listed in Organic Law, among them the certification of a matter as complex, and the ratification of an emergency decree under Article 4.5.3 may be voted remotely. For every remote session a civic room is open, which any member may use at their own choice. Final votes cast outside a session in person are cast in a protected civic booth, and a public terminal is provided to any member without connectivity. A body may raise its weekly share for a named complex matter within the limit of Organic Law, with release raised in proportion.

3. Anti-Entrenchment Mandate: No citizen may serve consecutive terms in the same tier; each mandate is followed by a hiatus of twice its length.

4. Prevention of Shadow Bureaucracies: Assembly staff serve on strictly temporary, rotating contracts; framing agendas, filtering citizen questions, or covertly influencing jurors is an impeachable offense. Agenda authority rests with sortition delegates.

5. Petition Intake and Deliberative Capacity: Any person may publish any petition on the open civic register, unfiltered. Agenda placement requires co-endorsement by a fixed share of the assembly's constituents; each person holds an equal, periodically renewed endorsement allowance, never conditioned on labor, conduct, or opinion. Each assembly fixes finite agenda slots per session, filled by public lottery weighted by distinct support. A rotating Triage Panel of three (3) citizens drawn by lot merges identical requests and checks form, never merit. Parameters are fixed by Organic Law.

6. **Second Reading by a Renewed Council:** A sixty percent (60%) decision of a Neighborhood Sortition Council may be re-examined once, on request made within thirty (30) days of its adoption by the endorsement procedure of Article 4.3.5. The re-examination is held by the same Council at its first session after the lot has replaced the number of the members who adopted the decision fixed by Organic Law. The decision stays in force meanwhile; only a decision certified irreversible under a closed list of Organic Law waits, and certification is decided within fourteen (14) days by a panel of three (3) citizens drawn under the rule for outside panels of Article 4.2, failing which the decision takes effect. A decision confirmed on second reading cannot be re-examined again; it remains open to repeal by an ordinary new decision. No new body and no plebiscite is created for this. This Article does not apply to bodies of one hundred and one (101) or more, to emergency decrees, or to certified routine maintenance.

##### **Article 4.4 — Drawn Expert Evidence and Intelligible Decision-Making**

1. Sortition jurors deliberate as the ethical conscience of the commons; technical advisors serve strictly in an evidentiary, non-voting capacity.

2. **Drawn Evidence Panels:** On complex proposals, an Evidence Panel is drawn by lot from the Practitioner Register of Article 4.4.6, free from corporate grants or debt. The jurors name the subject matters the proposal involves; any party may ask, before the draw, that one be added. The draw applies only the following filters, and publishes them with its result: subject matter, drawing from the categories named; place, a fixed share of seats from the bioregion concerned and a fixed share from outside it, set by Organic Law; hiatus, excluding anyone in hiatus for that subject matter under Article 4.3.3; conflict of interest, excluding residents of the proposing node and of any node that filed a formal objection before the draw was announced, anyone who signed a contribution to the proposal, and anyone whose admission rests on evidence issued by either side; diversity of source, so that no two members are admitted on evidence from the same school or the same attesting party. Residence is checked by a proof that reveals only that the person does not reside in the excluded nodes. Language is never a filter: the panel works in writing, each member's text in their own language is the authentic version, and translations are made by interpreters drawn by lot from the Register under the same rules and annexed beside the originals. Each panel is drawn for one proposal and dissolved when it reports; no standing expert body exists, and no member of a panel holds any office by reason of having served. Continuity rests on the published briefs of earlier sessions: a panel on a matter already briefed states what it confirms and what it revises in the earlier brief, and why. Service on an Evidence Panel is civic service under Article 4.3.2: members receive release from routine labor in proportion to the hours served, and replacement of domestic and care support for those hours. After reporting, each member observes the hiatus of Article 4.3.3 for that subject matter, counted on the length of the panel's service. Where the filtered pool is below the minimum of Organic Law, filters are relaxed in this order only, each step logged: place shares, then diversity of source, then hiatus. The conflict-of-interest filter is never relaxed.

3. **Practical Intelligibility and Disagreement Map:** The Evidence Panel files a plain-language brief stating the consequences, trade-offs and failure scenarios of the proposal and of inaction, what its members agree on, where they differ, and which observation would change each member's position. Each member signs individually; dissent is annexed in full and given the same length and place as the majority text.

4. **Juror-Led Inquiry:** Proponents and opponents then speak as declared parties. Jurors conduct the questioning, may call further witnesses, and deliberate without experts or parties present; written questions during deliberation are answered on the public record. Procedure follows the Fact-Centric Inquiry of Article 8.4.3.

5. **Precautionary Invariant:** Approval of a novel structural intervention is withheld where the record shows a specific, plausible pathway to irreversible harm to life or ecosystems that the proposal does not close (*In dubio pro natura et vita*). Confusion or disagreement alone never suspends approval. Suspension never interrupts certified routine maintenance or critical life support. Persistent disputes of fact pass to a fresh Evidence Panel from non-contiguous watersheds, which reports; the jurors decide.

6. **The Practitioner Register:** A single Practitioner Register serves every assembly, with one set of admission criteria for all applicants in every place; no local, bioregional or parallel register is created. Subject categories are listed in Organic Law, and anyone may propose a new one by petition under Article 4.3.5. Entry is voluntary: an applicant publishes, by their own choice, evidence of their work, and the Register never gathers evidence about a person on its own initiative (Article 3.4.4). Evidence is a signed, tamper-evident public record (Articles 7.3, 7.4) of one of three kinds, all on the same footing: a verifiable work, such as an open design with its history or an installation that exists; an attestation signed by the party that received the work, never by peers; a diploma recorded by the school that issued it. No kind is required; any kind that meets the criteria suffices, and the absence of one kind never excludes an applicant who meets them by another. Criteria are written in advance in Organic Law, countable and documentary, never judgments of quality; the thresholds for the three kinds are enacted together for each category. Attestations exchanged between two applicants in the same category do not count. No title, guild, association or professional body is required for admission or may gatekeep it. Each living person holds at most one entry, proven by Zero-Knowledge Proof-of-Personhood (Article 7.4.4); an entry may be held under a pseudonym, and a name is published only by the person's choice. An open model meeting Article 7.2 compares the filed evidence with the written criteria and returns only 'meets' or 'to be verified', citing for each criterion the document and passage it relied on; signatures, dates and counts are checked by ordinary auditable code. 'Meets' admits the entry, which stays open to challenge. 'To be verified' sends the filing to a jury of citizens drawn by lot from outside the field, which decides. No model rejects an entry or decides a challenge (Articles 7.1.2, 7.5.3); where no model meets Article 7.2, every filing goes to the jury. An entry becomes eligible for draws after a waiting period counted from filing, whatever the route, and never for a proposal published before it was filed. Anyone may challenge an entry once on each ground. A challenge is decided by a jury of citizens drawn by lot from outside the field, which rules only on whether the filed evidence is authentic and meets the written criteria, never on the quality of the person or of their opinions; the entry stays eligible while the challenge is pending. Entries, model outputs, challenges and rulings are logged publicly. The Register is replicated across watersheds, its signing keys are dispersed under Article 7.4, and any replica cut off from the others can still run a verifiable draw. The Register is separate from the civic register.

##### **Article 4.5 — Deliberative Protocols and Graduated Voting Thresholds**

Deliberative assemblies vote exclusively through graduated thresholds:

1. **75% Constitutional Supermajority:** Required exclusively for structural amendments to this Constitution, expansions (never diminutions, Article 1.7) of Invariant Rights, declarations of de-commodification, modifications to the biological survival baseline, or restorative measures under Article 1.8.6.

2. **60% Qualified Majority:** Required for multi-year bioregional development plans, foundational resource allocations, inter-watershed treaties, and the enactment of general regulatory statutes.

3. **50%+1 Simple Majority:** Permitted exclusively for routine administrative scheduling, procedural assembly motions, and immediate emergency disaster responses. Emergency decrees passed by simple majority carry an automatic, unextendable thirty (30) day sunset clause, requiring subsequent ratification under the sixty percent (60%) qualified threshold.

4. **Threshold Arithmetic:** Every percentage threshold denotes the smallest whole number of votes equal to or exceeding that share of all seated members, not merely of those present (e.g. 9 and 12 of 15; 61 and 76 of 101). In the assemblies of all adult residents of Article 4.2.1, thresholds are counted on votes cast, with the quorum fixed by Organic Law.

##### **Article 4.6 — Constitutional Review (*Custodes Constitutionis*)**

1. Any citizen, assembly, or Guardian of the Biosphere may petition for review of any enactment, decree, algorithm, or Organic Law alleged to violate this Constitution.

2. A single-case Constitutional Review Jury (*Custodes Constitutionis*) of one hundred and one (101) citizens, drawn by lot from watersheds not party to the dispute and deliberating under Article 4.4, voids an act by a sixty percent (60%) qualified majority, resolving doubts under Chapter I in favor of the person. Each party may appeal once to a fresh jury of three hundred and one (301) citizens.

3. Review juries declare conformity or nullity only; they may neither draft nor amend law.

2. **Drawn Evidence Panels:** On complex proposals, an Evidence Panel is drawn by lot from the Practitioner Register, free from corporate grants or debt.
   * *Subject matters:* the panel covers the subject matters the jurors name; any party may ask, before the draw, that one be added.
   * *Filters:* the draw applies only filters of subject matter, place, hiatus, conflict of interest and diversity of source, defined by Organic Law on countable facts, never on opinion, and published with its result; the conflict-of-interest filter is never relaxed. Residence is proven without revealing anything else.
   * *Language:* language is never a filter; each member's own text is authentic and drawn interpreters translate it.
   * *Term:* a panel serves one proposal and is dissolved when it reports; no standing expert body exists and no office follows from service, which is civic service under Article 4.3.2. Members observe the hiatus of Article 4.3.3 for that subject matter.

6. **The Practitioner Register:** One Practitioner Register serves every assembly; no parallel register exists, and it is separate from the civic register.
   * *Criteria:* one set of admission criteria applies, written in advance, countable and documentary, never judgments of quality. Categories are listed in Organic Law; anyone may propose one by petition.
   * *Entry:* entry is voluntary and the Register never gathers evidence about a person on its own initiative (Article 3.4.4). A verifiable work, an attestation by the party that received the work, never by peers, and a diploma recorded by its school stand on the same footing, with thresholds enacted together; none is required and any one suffices. No title, guild or professional body gatekeeps admission.
   * *Identity:* each person holds at most one entry, proven without revealing identity, and may hold it under a pseudonym; a name is published only by choice.
   * *Decisions:* no model rejects an entry or decides a challenge (Articles 7.1.2, 7.5.3); doubtful filings and challenges go to juries drawn from outside the field, which rule only on the evidence and the criteria, never on opinions. Anyone may challenge an entry, which stays eligible while the challenge is pending. No entry serves a proposal published before it was filed.
   * *Record:* entries, model outputs, challenges and rulings are public. The Register is replicated so that no single site or key controls it.
