#### A. The Collapse of Altruistic Reciprocity (Spiteful Dynamics & Free-Riding)

- The productive citizens performing sewer work observe the non-cooperative bloc receiving identical calories, water, and medical care while lounging in Tier 1 accommodation.

- Cooperators experience "sucker's payoff," leading to widespread voluntary defection. The labor pool contracts exponentially.

#### B. The Automation Fallacy as an Escape Hatch
Article 5.1.4 treats full automation as an inevitable panacea. However, maintenance of dirty, wet, unstructured biological systems (e.g., clearing fatbergs in legacy sewers, sorting contaminated organic matter) requires complex, high-entropy machine maintenance. If the humans tasked with maintaining the robots shirk via the Caregiver Immunity loop, the automation infrastructure degrades. The clause requiring plants to be *throttled or decommissioned* rather than using coercive pressure guarantees that mechanical entropy outpaces automated substitution.

#### C. The Toothlessness of "Metabolic Decoupling"
Article 5.2.2 (Stage Four) attempts to punish non-reciprocity by cutting off three-phase power, surplus compute, and workshop loops, leaving only the "Tier 1 Survival Capsule." 
- For an evolutionary fitness maximizer, the Tier 1 Survival Capsule provides the biological requirements for somatic maintenance and reproductive output (caloric sufficiency, climate control, water, connectivity, healthcare).
- Because carceral detention, kinetic eviction, and somatic enforcement are unconstitutional invariants (Art. 1.2, Art. 1.7, Art. 5.2.2, Art. 8.5.1), the system has no instrument to dislodge non-working populations from valuable land or space.
