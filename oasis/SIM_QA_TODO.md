### **The 4-Stage Hardening Lifecycle**

1. **`[STAGE 1: HYPOTHESIS]`** — Problem and empirical friction registered in [`oasis/sim_resolutions_qa.md`](sim_resolutions_qa.md).
2. **`[STAGE 2: RED_STRESS_TEST]`** — Adversarial Red Team assault (RED-1 to RED-5) identifying hidden exploits, loopholes, or failure modes.
3. **`[STAGE 3: PURPLE_PATCH]`** — BLUE-CORE defense + PURPLE-PATCH compilation of the hardened, un-gameable statutory diff.
4. **`[STAGE 4: CANONICAL_MERGE]`** — Merged directly into the Living Constitution chapters and Foundation Statutes prior to the Foundation Genesis Lock.

## **INSTRUCTIONS FOR NEW ENTRIES**

When a new operational edge-case or simulation friction is discovered:
1. Assign next sequential ID (`SIM-QA-06`, etc.).
2. Document full problem and preliminary hypothesis in [`oasis/sim_resolutions_qa.md`](sim_resolutions_qa.md).
3. Add entry to this board under `STAGE 1: HYPOTHESIS`.
4. Run adversarial stress-test (Red vs. Blue) one docket at a time.
5. Compile Purple Patch and execute canonical merge.
6. **A docket may close with `RED_WINS_WITHDRAWN`:** if the Red Team prevails, the provision is withdrawn or narrowed rather than patched. Not every docket must end in new text.
7. **Evidence rule:** every quantitative claim must link a reproducible script and results file (`simone/benchmarks/`). Game-engine or AI-session numbers are labelled synthetic.
8. **Compression disclosure rule (2026-11-12, after SIM-QA-22):** shortening may remove words, never a point (right, duty, prohibition, institution, guarantee, threshold, protected group), without the maintainer's explicit decision. Every compressed draft lists each point it removes or weakens, with source location and proposed destination; the maintainer decides point by point (keep, move, drop); every drop is recorded here with date and reason. A compressed text without the list is not submitted. If a word ceiling cannot be met without dropping points, the conflict goes to the maintainer.
9. **Chapter ceiling (2026-11-13):** the concision ceiling is 600–1,000 words per chapter (raised from 900 by maintainer decision). Dockets written before this date cite 900 as the ceiling then in force.
10. **Placement test (2026-11-13, maintainer):** for every clause, ask: if a 60% majority changed it, could someone lose a right or a group gain power? If yes, it stays in the Constitution; if no, it moves to Organic Law. Rights, prohibitions, the existence and powers of bodies, and the voting thresholds stay in the Constitution. Numbers, percentages (other than voting thresholds), deadlines, lists, categories, step-by-step procedures, tools and technologies move to Organic Law, each with a cross-reference. Whenever a number moves, the Constitution keeps its bound (a floor or a ceiling the Organic Law cannot cross), and the ratchet rule applies: Organic Law may strengthen a guarantee, never weaken it. The test and the compression disclosure rule (instruction 8) apply together: a move removes no point, and every move is listed. First applied to Chapter IV (Y-13, `sim_resolutions_qa.md`, Chapter IV Placement Proposal).
10a. **Word ceilings (2026-11-13, maintainer decision, Y-16; in force).** *Replaces the paragraph waivers of SIM-QA-22 (Chapter V) and SIM-QA-23 (Chapter I), which are retired.* (1) Each chapter holds at most 1,000 words, counted as tokens containing a letter or a digit, headings included. (2) Chapter I holds at most 1,200 and Chapter IV at most 2,150 words; no other chapter has an own ceiling, and one is added only by a recorded maintainer decision after the whole chapter has been through the placement test and still exceeds 1,000 words with no point that can be moved. (3) The Constitution as a whole holds at most 11,500 words. (4) No docket raises the ceiling it needs; a ceiling changes only by a separate maintainer decision, with a recount of every chapter. (5) A chapter within fifty (50) words of its ceiling receives new detail in Organic Law by default; text the placement test keeps in the Constitution is never moved to meet a ceiling, and the conflict goes to the maintainer under instruction 8. (6) A ceiling is never met by dropping a point.
    *Maintainer decision 2026-11-20:* the figure of 1,200 for Chapter I in point (2) is **suspended until Chapter I is fully drafted**; the ceiling is then set by one decision at the counted length plus a small margin. Points (1) and (3) to (6) stay in force; the placement test and instruction 8 still apply to every Chapter I draft. The figure for Chapter IV (2,150) is not changed, although the recount finds it already passed ([`word_recount_2026-11-20.md`](word_recount_2026-11-20.md)).
    *Maintainer decision 2026-11-20 (second):* the figure of 11,500 for the whole in point (3) is **suspended until the open dockets are drafted**; a new total is then set by one decision on a full recount. Until then no docket raises a ceiling (point 4 stands), and **every draft states the count of its chapter and of the whole**, so that the next recount is a sum.
11. **Plain-language pass (2026-11-13, maintainer; proposed instruction)** `[STATUS: SIMULATION_HYPOTHESIS]`. Keep the structure, the numbering, the cross-references and the wording. Apply only five rules: (1) remove Latin tags; (2) remove intensifiers that add no rule (strictly, exclusively, invariant, inviolable, absolute, and similar); where the word carries a rule (for example a right that no majority may suspend under Art. 1.7), keep it; (3) replace ornate labels and titles with plain ones (for example "Rotation" for "Anti-Entrenchment Mandate"); (4) split any sentence longer than 30 words into two, keeping its words; (5) use one word for one thing across the whole Constitution, with one table of term replacements, decided at the first chapter where a term appears and reused in every later chapter ([`plain_language_pass.md`](plain_language_pass.md)). Nothing is removed because it is vague or has no stated consequence: such a phrase stays in the text and is recorded in [`restore_and_elaborate.md`](restore_and_elaborate.md) with the phrase, its location, what is missing (a definition, a consequence, who decides) and a one-line proposal; each entry is a future docket. Every place where two honest readers could reach different results goes in the same file, unresolved. Words removed under rules 1 to 3 are listed with their location, under the compression disclosure rule (instruction 8). No point is removed or weakened; if a rule cannot be applied without losing a point, the text stays as it is and the case is reported. English master text only; no translations, no Organic Law text.
