### **The 4-Stage Hardening Lifecycle**

1. **`[STAGE 1: HYPOTHESIS]`** — Problem and empirical friction registered in [`oasis/sim_resolutions_qa.md`](sim_resolutions_qa.md).
2. **`[STAGE 2: RED_STRESS_TEST]`** — Adversarial Red Team assault (RED-1 to RED-5) identifying hidden exploits, loopholes, or failure modes.
3. **`[STAGE 3: PURPLE_PATCH]`** — BLUE-CORE defense + PURPLE-PATCH compilation of the hardened, un-gameable statutory diff.
4. **`[STAGE 4: CANONICAL_MERGE]`** — Merged directly into the Living Constitution chapters and Foundation Statutes prior to the Foundation Genesis Lock.

## **INSTRUCTIONS FOR NEW ENTRIES**

When a new operational edge-case or simulation friction is discovered:
1. Assign next sequential ID (`SIM-QA-06`, etc.).
2. Document full problem and preliminary hypothesis in [`oasis/sim_resolutions_qa.md`](sim_resolutions_qa.md).
3. Add entry to this board under `STAGE 1: HYPOTHESIS`.
4. Run adversarial stress-test (Red vs. Blue) one docket at a time.
5. Compile Purple Patch and execute canonical merge.
6. **A docket may close with `RED_WINS_WITHDRAWN`:** if the Red Team prevails, the provision is withdrawn or narrowed rather than patched. Not every docket must end in new text.
7. **Evidence rule:** every quantitative claim must link a reproducible script and results file (`simone/benchmarks/`). Game-engine or AI-session numbers are labelled synthetic.
