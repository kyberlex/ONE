### **The 4-Stage Hardening Lifecycle**

1. **`[STAGE 1: HYPOTHESIS]`** — Problem and empirical friction registered in [`oasis/sim_resolutions_qa.md`](sim_resolutions_qa.md).
2. **`[STAGE 2: RED_STRESS_TEST]`** — Adversarial Red Team assault (RED-1 to RED-5) identifying hidden exploits, loopholes, or failure modes.
3. **`[STAGE 3: PURPLE_PATCH]`** — BLUE-CORE defense + PURPLE-PATCH compilation of the hardened, un-gameable statutory diff.
4. **`[STAGE 4: CANONICAL_MERGE]`** — Merged directly into the Living Constitution chapters and Foundation Statutes prior to the Foundation Genesis Lock.
