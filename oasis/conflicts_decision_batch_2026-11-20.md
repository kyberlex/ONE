## **Conflict 1. Withdrawal of a bioregion against standing solidarity** *(SIM-QA-35 point 5, D-35.4; SIM-QA-38 point 2, D-38.5)*

**Current text, Art. 10.3.3:**
> 3. Hoarding life-support resources, forming cartels, or cutting off downstream water triggers automatic Systemic Decoupling (*Disiunctio Reticularis*): immediate suspension of inter-basin grid interties, transit corridors, and advanced medical buffers until reciprocity is restored.

**Current text, Art. 10.1.4:**
> 4. Higher-tier assemblies possess zero jurisdiction to override local autonomy, save to halt exported pollution, protect shared aquifers, or defend invariant rights.

**SIM-QA-35, point 5:**
> A bioregion may withdraw from compacts, solidarity, higher assemblies and standards by 75% of its adult residents after notice. It cannot leave Chapter I, enclose the commons, or escape Art. 10.1.4.

**SIM-QA-38, point 2 (part):**
> Standing, unconditional for Tier 1 [...] Not shed by withdrawal, not suspended by decoupling, and counted as such in the audit of Art. 3.1.6.

**Problem.** If withdrawal sheds solidarity, the basins with a surplus leave and the floor of the poor basins falls. If nothing can be shed, withdrawal means little.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | No withdrawal of a bioregion at all; only the individual's exit. |
| **B** | Withdrawal as in SIM-QA-35 point 5, except that solidarity for Tier 1 is never shed: a withdrawn bioregion keeps giving and receiving it. Decoupling (Art. 10.3.3) never suspends Tier 1 solidarity in either direction. |
| **C** | Withdrawal sheds everything, solidarity included. |

*Red team:* **B.** Tier 1 is a Chapter I right of persons, not a favour between basins, so a vote of one basin cannot remove it from the persons of another. *Cost:* a withdrawn bioregion still owes, and nothing but decoupling enforces the debt, which B forbids for Tier 1 flows. The duty rests on consent in the end. *Canon touched:* Art. 10.3.3 gains an exception (weakened as a sanction: disclosure).

## **Conflict 3. A machine attends a person only with consent; a machine of restraint has none** *(SIM-QA-31 point 4, D-31.1; SIM-QA-37 point 3, D-37.5)*

**Current text, Art. 8.2.1:**
> 1. Public safety is embedded in spatial architecture: corridors and dwellings maintain daylight sightlines, eliminating entrapment zones. Automated machinery contains optical interlocks triggering kinetic shutdowns upon human approach.

**Current text, Art. 8.1.3 (first sentence):**
> 3. Universal Weapon Ban: Designing, manufacturing, or compiling weaponry, munitions, or robotics intended to kill or injure persons is categorically prohibited to humans and machines alike.

**SIM-QA-31, point 4:**
> The stop on human approach stays the rule; a class of machine may work near people only after outside certification for a stated setting; a machine attends a person only with consent, and never as the only care.

**SIM-QA-37, point 3:**
> Remotely operated, one operator each, short range, behind a force limit, activated with keys held by drawn citizens, few in number. Never acting alone.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | No machine of restraint. Only passive means: shields, barriers, doors. SIM-QA-31 point 4 and Art. 8.2.1 stand without exception. |
| **B** | Machines of restraint allowed with the guards of SIM-QA-37 point 3, as a stated exception to SIM-QA-31 point 4 and to Art. 8.2.1. |

*Red team:* **A.** A body that needs many people to coerce can be refused by them; a machine removes that brake (SIM-QA-37 RED-15), and a hijacked one is a weapon. *Cost of A:* against a person with a firearm, people stand behind shields and others may die before the person is held. The canon already accepts that risk in Art. 8.1.3.

> An interim stop on a practitioner is ordered by a panel of three drawn from outside, on facts of harm or demonstrated danger, with a short ceiling.

The criteria for a demonstration are adopted by the drawn body on rival evidence (SIM-QA-20), never written by the guild alone. |

## **Conflict 6. Training data public against no personal data in training** *(SIM-QA-31 point 3, D-31.1; SIM-QA-41 point 6, D-41.2)*

**SIM-QA-31, point 3:**
> Models used in common are commons: code, weights and training record public; personal data never in training without consent; releases by the vote of Art. 7.6.3; each node may hold a version back.

**SIM-QA-41, point 6 (last sentence):**
> No personal data in the training of a model.

**Problem.** One says "never without consent", the other "never". A public training record of personal data publishes that data for good; consent cannot be withdrawn from weights.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Never, with or without consent, for any model used in common. The training record is then public in full. |
| **B** | With consent; the record of that part is sealed. |

*Red team:* **A.** *Cost:* models that could learn from medical records (diagnosis) are not trained inside the commons; imported models of that kind work only behind the supervisor (SIM-QA-31 point 5). A person may still publish their own data as a person; it is then public data, not consented training data.

## **Conflict 9. Legal organs answer in law; drawn bodies decide under the Constitution** *(SIM-QA-40 point 6, D-40.8; SIM-QA-42 point 2, D-42.5)*

**Current text:** the canon has none; the legal organs exist only in the Statutes of the Foundation.

**SIM-QA-40, point 6:**

**SIM-QA-42, point 2 (part):**
> Removal decided outside the Board on the drawn panel's proposal. The panel's assent for the gravest acts.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | The Board carries out the decisions of drawn bodies unless it finds one unlawful. A refusal is published with its reason. A dispute goes to a panel drawn from outside, which hears counsel; its finding is published and may ground removal under SIM-QA-42 point 2. In law the Board keeps the last word on what it signs. |

## **Conflict 10. Proof needs records; the data rule removes them** *(SIM-QA-33 RED-8, D-33.2; SIM-QA-41, D-41.2; SIM-QA-32 point 4)*

**Current text, Art. 8.4.3:**
> 3. **Stage 3 (Sortition Arbitration Tribunal):** Binding adjudication by a Citizen Tribunal of fifteen (15) citizens via Fact-Centric Inquiry (*Inquisitio Facti*). Adversarial courtroom trials are abolished: proceedings evaluate strictly verifiable physical evidence and open logs.

**Current text, Art. 8.2.2 (last sentence):**
> Public sensors shall never record speech nor compile biometric registries.

**SIM-QA-33, point 2:**
> Any person may file; a panel of three drawn from outside checks form and a minimum of evidence and gathers records from bodies and guilds, never from a person's own devices.

**SIM-QA-41, point 3:**
> Proof held by the person for shifts, hiatus and allowances; no body keeps the record.

| # | Question | Options | Red team |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **10a** | Testimony (D-33.2) | **A** keep "physical evidence and open logs" only; unrecorded acts go unproven. **B** admit testimony for the listed non-physical acts, only with corroboration by a record or a second independent source; never testimony alone. | B |
| **10b** | Which records exist | The records of what bodies and guilds do (work logs, decisions, allocations: SIM-QA-32 point 4) are kept and public. Records about a person are held by that person. A person may give their own records and messages as evidence; nobody is made to. **Confirm** or change. | confirm |

*On 10a.* Under A, stalking, threats and most harms between persons cannot be proven at all, and the list of SIM-QA-33 is words. B opens false accusation; corroboration is the guard and it is not strong. *Canon touched:* Art. 8.4.3 is **weakened** ("strictly verifiable physical evidence"): compression disclosure, a decision of its own.
*On 10b.* The line is between acts of bodies (recorded) and lives of persons (not recorded). It leaves one gap: a guild member's work log is both.

## **Conflict 11. The audit of Art. 3.1.6 fails every node that imports or receives** *(SIM-QA-31 RED-9, SIM-QA-35 H4, SIM-QA-38; D-38.6)*

**Current text, Art. 3.1.6:**
> 6. Tier 1 Autarkic Closure: Viability requires more than non-negative gross output: an annual public audit must show that all sectors directly or indirectly supplying Tier 1 needs, including shelter and healthcare, meet full Tier 1 demand from local resources and installed capacity alone, with external inputs at zero and a published reserve margin.

**Current text, Art. 10.3.2 (second sentence):**
> Dense urban agglomerations operate as specialized metabolic organs under bilateral compacts, trading advanced industrial outputs and scientific research for food and energy surpluses.

**Problem.** A seed node imports machines, a poor basin receives solidarity, a city trades for its food. On the words, each fails the audit every year.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Closure stays a condition, as written. Seed nodes, cities and recipients are failed nodes by the audit. |
| **B** | Closure is the goal the audit measures. The audit publishes the gap. Three kinds of input are counted as what they are and are not a failure: certified solidarity for Tier 1; the compacts of Art. 10.3.2 at the level where they close (the bioregion, not the city); imports during the transition, with a plan to close. A machine counts only as far as it is locally maintainable (SIM-QA-31 point 6). Failure is defined by SIM-QA-35 point 6: Tier 1 in fact not delivered. |
| **C** | The audit is taken at the bioregion only, never at a node. |

*Red team:* **B.** The canon already contradicts itself between Arts. 3.1.6 and 10.3.2. *Cost:* Art. 3.1.6 is **weakened** from a condition to a measure with exceptions (compression disclosure); an exception that is certified every year becomes permanent dependence, so each needs an outside certification and a term. The level of closure and the donor share are `NOT_RUN` (SIMONE, SIM-QA-38 RED-3 and RED-4): no figure may be written before those runs.

## **Conflict 12. Who answers when a guild or a machine harms** *(SIM-QA-31 point 7, D-31.1; SIM-QA-32 point 1, D-32.1; SIM-QA-33 point 5, D-33.1)*

**Current text, Art. 8.3.3:**
> 3. Somatic Restitution: The community guarantees immediate trauma therapy and material restitution prior to allocating resources toward transgressor rehabilitation.

**SIM-QA-31, point 7:**
> Every working machine has a named steward that answers for harm.

**SIM-QA-32, point 1 (part):**
> It holds no authority of its own [...] The mandate has a term and can be given to another group on stated grounds.

**SIM-QA-33, point 5:**
> An act of a body is voided under Art. 4.6; no member answers for a vote.

| Option | Content |
| :--- | :--- |
| **A** | Three layers. *Reparation:* the commons repairs at once, whoever is at fault (Art. 8.3.3), and the harmed person never has to find someone to blame. *The steward:* every machine and every mandate has a named steward, which is a body or a guild, never one person; it answers by review of the facts and by loss of the mandate or of the machine's certification. *The person:* answers only for their own act on the closed list (SIM-QA-33); never for a vote, never for a machine's fault while following the adopted procedure. |
| **B** | A named individual answers for each machine and each mandate. |
| **C** | No steward; the commons repairs and nobody answers. |

*Red team:* **A.** B makes one person carry a system's fault and nobody will take the role; C removes every reason to maintain. *Residual:* a diffuse fault (a bad adopted rule, followed correctly) leaves the harm repaired and nobody answering, by design.
